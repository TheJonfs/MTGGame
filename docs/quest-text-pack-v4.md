# Quest & Rumor Text Pack v4 — the Heart's voice and the Chronicle of Cuttings (planner-authored; S27)

*The v1 register holds. Every line is written to survive any eventual disposition — the flower is never said to want, to hunger, or to grieve; it is only ever observed. The Manafleur's name appears on its card face and in the Chronicle's ledger header, nowhere else.*

## The Heart's door
- **Open (five petals fallen):** "The petals are gone. What they grew from is waiting."
- **The telegraph at the Heart:** "Here the currents meet. Five laws fell to you, and the thing that grew them has not fallen — it will grow them again as fast as you can cut them, one each night, in the order the plane runs. It has a name. You will read it on the card, and nowhere else. The heart does not offer terms."
- **The stakes line (zero ante):** "Nothing is wagered here. The flower has nothing to lose that it cannot regrow, and you have already given the road everything it asked."

## The Manafleur (card face)
- **Flavor text:** "Five petals. Five laws. Five who thought the laws were theirs. Beneath them all, the flower — which never noticed it was being ruled."

## The ceremonies
- **Victory (the postponement):** "The flower folds. The laws fall silent in the same breath, and the currents slow and feel for their old beds. This is not an ending. A petal closes; the world remembers who closed it. For now." — then: "Its card is yours. There is exactly one."
- **Loss at the Heart:** "The flower stands. You are left at the heart, where nothing presses you. Rest, and return; it will be here."
- **The offer after victory:** "Stay in the quiet world, or begin again on a new road — carrying what you carried out."

## The Chronicle of Cuttings (the profile's ledger; one entry per Manafleur victory, keyed to the run's starting road; the count is a running header — "The first cutting," "The second cutting"…)
- **From the white road:** "Cut from the white road: the flower folded, and for a season the pale country's wounds did not close on their own. Whatever the flower was doing, it was doing that too."
- **From the blue road:** "Cut from the blue road: the tide at the Spire ran forward for the first time anyone remembers. Then it thought better of it."
- **From the black road:** "Cut from the black road: the barrows were quiet, and then they were not quiet in a new way. The dead have opinions about flowers."
- **From the red road:** "Cut from the red road: the Caldera cooled a hand's breadth. Ash fell upward for a day."
- **From the green road:** "Cut from the green road: the Deepwood grew a ring of new trunks overnight, every one of them leaning toward the heart."
- **The fifth cutting (all five roads held — the phase-two turn):** "Five cuttings. The flower folds slower each time, and each time it folds, something under it turns over — like a sleeper deciding which side to face. The currents no longer run where they ran. Whatever comes next will not be Cinquefoil as you knew it."

## The new road (the new-game screen, when a legacy is carried)
- "You have cut the flower from the {colour} road. You begin with what you carried out: {power}, {guardian}, and {minister}."
- *(All five held:)* "You have cut the flower from every road. You begin with everything you carried out — and the plane has noticed."

## Small lines
- **The petal drop withheld (minister already held):** "Its minister is already yours. The petal yields coin instead, and the two currents it kept."
- **The Chronicle page header:** "The Chronicle of Cuttings — the Manafleur, and what followed each folding."
