# Token art: snail_0_3_wu (Snail Token — Whelkin's)

Card-art class (the token precedent: our own art, house style like the snake,
sphinx and beast tokens). Whelkin, the white-blue High Ground legend, makes two
0/3 white and blue Snail creature tokens when it enters or attacks; the token is
a small cousin of the great purple-shelled marsh snail on Whelkin's own card.
Rendered 1:1, center-cropped to the frame's 5:4 art window. S73a (Chris,
2026-11-19).

A single sturdy marsh snail the size of a hound, seen from the side at the
