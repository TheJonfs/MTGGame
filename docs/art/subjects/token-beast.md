# Token art: beast_4_4_g (Beast Token)

Card-art class (S7 feedback round / S17 Expansion 1: tokens get our own art
rather than Scryfall pulls — they are custom defs, so oracle grounding
doesn't apply). House style like the goblin/soldier/bear/wurm tokens. Rendered
1:1, center-cropped to the frame's 5:4 art window (35% upward bias).

Post-S53 (Chris): the one creature token without art — Rampaging Baloths'
"create a 4/4 green Beast creature token". Kept distinct from the bestiary's
Rumbling Baloth plate (a plated, tusked charger): this one is shaggy and horned,
standing its ground.

## Visual descriptor (locked)

```text
A single great green forest beast standing its ground and bellowing, seen
from slightly below: a massive shaggy body on four thick legs, a mane of
moss-green fur over a humped back, two broad curving horns swept forward
from a heavy brow, breath steaming from its open mouth, ferns and broken
saplings crushed under its hooves. Deep woodland light breaking through the
canopy behind it, no rider, no people. The beast fills the center of the
composition, head and horns in the upper half.
```
