**ADR-091 — The gauntlet's structure: the flower's laws, the Mirror, the petal-world.**

*Story canon (Chris-ratified):* **the laws were the flower's** — the per-battle re-injection of stronghold laws is lore: laws regrow because they were grown, five petals of the Bloom's own law worn by sovereigns who mistook blossoms for crowns. No shipped mechanic changes; the ending's meaning is set. (The full story record: `the-bloom-gauntlet-v1.md` §1–2.)

*The Vault and the Mirror (Chris-conceived; planner additions Chris-ratified):* locked until all five Moxen are held; inside, **the player's own deck played against them** — the ladder's self-play mirrors surfaced as content — **plus the Black Lotus in the copy** (the reflection fights with the prize), **ante off both ways** (a reflection has nothing to lose; mirror-ante would mint duplicates), **no guardian card** (the encounter's identity is the reflection). Prize: Black Lotus, `prizeOnly`, at last with an address. Placement: the outer map's center (a second door beside the Corolla's) unless Chris moves it inside the flower's town — his call in the round.

*The Corolla as a world (Chris-conceived):* the final dungeon is **a small radial world map — five petals around a central town — the logo rendered as geography**, entered from the outer center on five seals. Petal tips carry **fixed-point boss fights** (the ADR-067 lair pattern) — each still-pair signature's deck under its chamber's returned law; signature cards drop sole-mechanism as the lords' did. **The Heart battle sits at the town**, door locked until five petals fall; its fight and the Bloom's card remain undesigned — Chris noodles; out of S26's scope. Planner proposals for the round: world-kind rules (pay-as-you-go, persistence, walk-out-and-return), no roamers, the empowerment knob shipped off, the town's baseline shell (inn, the R-drawer shelf, tavern, the Heart's door) pending Chris's offerings.

*The Corolla's five decks* drafted v1 in the gauntlet doc around Chris's skeletons; petal-sim (guardian-sim's pattern with the law in play) tunes them.

*Sequencing:* **S26 builds everything but the Heart** — the still-pair batch, the petal-world, the Vault and Mirror, the decks and instruments. The Heart fight, the victory text, and the chronicle's first lines follow the Bloom's card in its own session.
