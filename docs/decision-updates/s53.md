## Measured (Part 0)

- **ADR-154, as ruled, moves little and costs a little.** Sealed seed 49, the same rating, 200 pools: blue's share of decks 67% → 63%, decking 17% → 16%, the colours' win rates 48–51% (49–51% with the best pair). Head to head, the varied-pair decks win **48.0% ± 0.8** against best-pair decks (the same pool read both ways: 45.1%). Blue sits in most pools' top three pairs (UW, UB, UG), so a choice over three rarely leaves it; the second and third pairs cost about two points a deck. Applied wherever the Sealed field builds (the single Sealed event's AI seats and a Convocation's Sealed stage). *Rule on*: keep it, weight it by score difference rather than raw score, or take blue's dominance to the pool.
