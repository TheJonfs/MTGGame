## Part 1 — combat reads the defender's triggers (books 141–142)
- **`triggercharge`**: the attack search charges each attacker the battlefield's other permanents' ATTACKS triggers as it is declared (one they kill deals nothing and is lost); the block planner weighs each blocker after the BLOCKS triggers (one they kill still blocks, for no damage); the crack-back read leaves out their attackers our triggers kill. `declarationCharge` reads the effect vocabulary — any trigger on another permanent that damages the declared creature — so a future card with one is read the same way.
- **`deathcharge`**: an own creature's death in the attack sim also costs the life the opponent's death triggers drain (Blood Artist 1, the Usher 2, Poison-Tip Archer 1), at the archetype's own-life weight.

## Part 2 — damage targeting (books 143–144)
- **`burnkills`**: a burn spell (an instant or sorcery whose effect damages its target) is aimed at an opposing creature only when it kills — its damage (an X spell's X; Sacred Helix's mana spent) with what is already committed: our pending spells and pings on the stack at it, and once blocks are declared the combat damage our blockers or attackers deal it. The face, or holding, scored as before. **Why it happened**: a non-lethal burn spell is "unchanged" to the predictor and scored a quarter-point under passing — a lower score, not a gate, so the softmax cast it now and then.
- **`pingplan`**: a repeatable ping at an opposing creature counts our pings already on the stack (a target they already kill is not pinged again — the Tyrant's doubled ping at a Witch); a ping short of a kill is spent only when the untapped mana and the life at hand (the Tyrant's 1 a ping) pay for the rest of the kill this turn (two pings on a 4-toughness Clio). **Finding**: the brief's "a single non-lethal ping is refused as now" held for lands only — a creature's ping (the Tyrant) had no gate at all, its non-lethal pings left to the softmax; it now has this one.
- **Measured** (Grixis, 160 matches a pairing, paired against both off): the Rabble +0.6 ± 0.3 (+2.3 ± 0.8 against the three Clio lists), the Kindling +1.0 ± 0.6, the Tally +1.2 ± 0.7, **the Kiln +0.5 ± 0.7 overall, +6.9 ± 1.5 against the Clio lists** (its Tyrant now stacks two on Clio). ## Part 3 — the Witch, early while it is safe (book 145)
- **`witchearly`**: outside a combo plan's own dig, life is paid for cards from the turn it is safe — the life left above the opponent's board reach (every creature that can attack us) plus three a burn spell in their graveyard **plus a cushion of six**, with no racer of theirs (power two or more) that none of our creatures can block and survive; a hand of seven does not draw; book 113's burn floor stays the stop. The S27 rule (draw only at a hand of two) is what it replaces; book 102's pin of that rule re-baselined to run with `witchearly` off.
