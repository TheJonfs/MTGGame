# Token art: wurm_4_4 (Wurm Token)

Card-art class (S7 feedback round / S17 Expansion 1: tokens get our own art
rather than Scryfall pulls — they are custom defs, so oracle grounding
doesn't apply). House style like the goblin/soldier tokens. Rendered 1:1,
center-cropped to the frame's 5:4 art window.

## Visual descriptor (locked)

```text
A single green wurm rearing up from broken forest floor, seen from

a blunt wedge head with a wide jaw, moss and torn roots clinging to its

people. The coiled body fills the lower half, the raised head the upper
```
