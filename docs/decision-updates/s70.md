## Book 152 — `groundtiming` (Part 2)
`HeuristicAgent.groundTimingGated` (inside `floodSinkGated`, for a land's ability with a mana cost). An ability that damages, destroys, searches, copies or mills waits for the opponent's end step. A creature target may die sooner if it is attacking or blocking now, or if, before our attack (main one, beginning of combat, an empty stack), it is untapped and could block a creature of ours that is ready to swing. Damage at a player keeps the S40 rule (a lethal shot at any time). The land sacrifice (Burnfallow (b)) has two more guards: never with no basic left in the library (the decklist less the basics seen), and never a land that is a colour's last source. The land given up is a basic of the colour held most, never one with an ability beyond mana. Nightbower at X=0 (a token, a Mox) is no longer refused by the X=0 gate (`xCeilingTarget`). Pins: book 152 (`book-of-shame.test.ts`). **The rule also times the Fordkeeper's granted land pings** ({2}, {T}: 1 damage), so the Tollgate's baselines moved (41.3 → 44.6) and the second run re-ran every baseline.

**Ladder: gate/mirror PASS** (`analysis/s70/ladder-book152.log`).
