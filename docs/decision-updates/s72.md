## Part 2 — Mystic Snake (concern 30): the probe
- **The probe**: `pnpm counter-log-probe <saved games…> --list <key> [--card mystic_snake]` (`world/counter-log-probe-cli.ts`; S56's `counter-probe` plays its own games, this one reads saved logs). It replays each saved game and reads every priority window of the list's seat. A window is one turn, step and stack.
  - For each window it records whether the Snake was castable (among the legal actions) and whether it was cast.
  - Each window is classed by moment:
    - a counter window: an opponent's spell on top;
    - their end step with an empty stack;
    - our main phase with an empty stack (a body);
    - other.

  It also records the mana value of each spell countered.
- **The games**: `open:rr --save-games`, 20 matches a pairing, seed 91, in `analysis/s72/snake/`:

- **Concern 30 was a measurement error.** The 11-10 scan (`analysis/bodies/cs-mardu-scan.json`) credited a counter to the spell that resolved one, and the Snake's counter is its enters trigger. So the scan found 3 counters in 117 casts, where the same games hold 110. The AI already plays the Snake as the brief's rule wants:
  - it is held, and cast into an opponent's spell in 98–100% of the windows it can be;
  - it is never cast into an empty main phase;
  - it counters three-drops and up three times in four (mana value 1–2 is about one in six).

  Countersnake's tables do not undercount the list on this count.
- **The one clause of `snakehold` the pilot does not play**: the brief's "at the opponent's end step when no target came". In 124 windows across the four sets, the Snake was castable at their end step with nothing on the stack and was never cast. Its first cast comes late, at a mean of turn 13–20. Whether casting it there as a body, when the mana would otherwise idle, beats keeping the counter is unmeasured. **For the planner, with Chris:** it is a trial of a new behaviour, not a correction.

## Part 2 — The baseline leak (concern 32): the census
- **The census**: `pnpm leak-census` (`world/leak-census-cli.ts`; report `analysis/s72/leak/census.md`). It runs every instant and sorcery cast from hand (each mode), and every activated ability of a permanent on our side, through the master pilot's scorer on two boards:
  - a **null board**: our ten basics, their three Swamps, empty graveyards, an empty stack;
  - the same board with a **Grizzly Bears a side**, for the spells whose target must be a creature.

  Each target the boards offer is a variant: a player, a land, a Bears. A variant is a **no-op** when its predicted board, less the card's own move and the source's tap, is the board it started from. Effects the view does not show (a library search, an extra turn) are never read as no-ops.
- **Reach**: 263 variants of 69 cards, out of 123 with a spell or a non-mana ability. Gates refused 135 variants outright (−∞). The 54 cards not reached need a stack spell, a graveyard card or an artifact to target, or act from hand or a graveyard. On a board that offers their target they act on it. The shapes where a legal target does nothing are each already gated:
  - our own spell for a counter (post-S43);
  - a same-side exchange (book 165);
  - a pump whose kill fails (book 137, `misfire`);
  - an Edict into no creatures (S32).
- **45 no-ops; none is a leak.**
  - Every one scores under passing except Thought Replacement at them (+0.20). That is book 153's caster's pick, priced on purpose at 0.6 (`CASTER_PICK_WORTH`): the hand count nets to nothing, but their best spell is taken. It is a quality trade, not a no-op.
  - The others:
    - Correspondence, a same-side exchange: −2.40 (book 165 at work);
    - Arcanis's return to hand: −0.25 (the friction);
    - the discards and the Helix at our own head: about −100 (the misaim cliff).
  - No soft leaks (within 0.25 under passing).
- **For the planner**: no further book from the census. Book 165's fix was the one leak of its shape that the pool holds.
