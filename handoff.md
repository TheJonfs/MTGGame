## Concerns

1. **The +8% master target is not reachable with constant-search over this evaluator** (DoD 2's plateau branch). Temperature bought +3.8 (S9); searched constants bought 0 more (held-out). The reason is structural: at affordable games-per-evaluation (~800), the objective's noise floor (±1.8%) is about the size of any single constant's true effect, so descent finds seed-fit, not signal — more search would find more overfit. **My 2-ply recommendation: yes, but surgically** — per the brief's escalation clause, 2-ply on high-stakes decisions only (removal targeting, alpha-strike sizing, sweeper timing — decisions where the top-2 scores are within ~1 mana unit and a real consequence differs). That's where journeyman's one-ply predictions are actually wrong, and it's bounded compute. A full-width 2-ply would blow the AI-delay budget in play mode.
2. **E's difficulty ladder is locally inverted:** apprentice-E (never holds, plays aggro) beats journeyman-E on the play (51.0% at 1,000/cell). Together with Part 2's plateau this says E's remaining weakness is that *holding itself* — not just when-behind holding — is overpriced on the play. Lead for the planner: an on-the-play/on-the-draw posture input, or pricing held mana against the tempo of the unplayed threat, both new evaluator input shapes needing ADR approval.
3. **Deterrence's sane-relative cost is real (−0.8 to −2.5 on some cells) and accepted by ADR-060 as the human-challenge trade** — but the human half is unverified until Chris plays. The concrete before/after moment to look for: a lone Typhoid Rats (or Deadly Recluse) now stays home against a bigger board instead of chipping 1 and dying — but still swings when you're at ≤10 life or its board is winning the race. If master still over-attacks in his next game, save the log to `results/playtest/`.
4. **ADR-014 makes legal-but-empty block steps invisible** (the menace report). Narration now explains it, but if Chris wants certainty over speed, a "always pause at declare blockers while I control any untapped creature" per-step stop is a ~5-line controller change — needs his verdict on pacing vs control.
5. **The `gate/aggregate` FAIL line is a standing confusion** — it's printed by `formatLadder` but hasn't been a ratified gate since S8 established deck imbalance dominates pairing cells. Suggest the planner either renames it ("info/per-deck majority") or drops it from the report so future sessions don't re-investigate it (I did, again, this session).
6. **The stop-on-opponent-spell pauses twice when you CAN respond** (once to look, once when your real priority window arrives) — only the first pause is the new one; if that reads as friction, the fix is to skip the look-pause when the upcoming window will be meaningful, but that needs the enumeration one step early. Chris's verdict after play.
7. **Ranked remaining AI tells** (DoD 4, my playtest-2 watchlist, most human-visible first): (1) removal timing — fires Doom Blade at the best *current* target instead of holding for the known bigger threat in your list; (2) no memory of revealed information — after Duress shows your hand it still plays as if only the decklist is known; (3) greedy per-creature blocks — never double-blocks a big attacker with two smalls except for menace; (4) trigger-target picks are unclassifiable (S7 escalation) and occasionally read as random; (5) Blaze sizing is myopic — casts at affordable X rather than saving for lethal reach; (6) equipment host choice is sometimes suboptimal. (1) and (3) are the ones a human notices in a single game; both are 2-ply-shaped problems, reinforcing Concern 1's recommendation.
