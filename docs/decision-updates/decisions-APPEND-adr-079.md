*Roadmap amendment (Chris-ruled):* **S20 = duals + the dungeon system + the five Mox dungeons; S21 = sieges + the retrieval and rumor-chain quest shapes (unblocked by dungeons); S22 = strongholds + the five custom bosses; S23 = endgame gauntlet + wilds polish.** Rationale recorded: strongholds are dungeons at maximum scale; the Mox guardians (real legendaries, real decks) are the cheap first pass at the boss-and-treasure loop before the expensive custom five spend on it.

*The dungeon design is ratified per `docs/dungeon-design.md` v2* — binding highlights: dungeons are **mini-worlds** on the existing map stack; **the exterior clock freezes inside** (§5's clock-free interiors) while an **interior step counter** drives guardian empowerment on **discrete, visible, difficulty-parameterized tiers** (`dungeonEmpowermentTiers`; difficulty bundles override per principle 5; steps are the only empowerment input); **interior life persists across battles** (loss and gain) on a track seeded from world life and discarded at exit (§2a intact; interior losses still pay the world loss penalty); topology is branching/interconnecting with **stationary chokepoint minions and no interior parley**; exit is victory, walk-out, or defeat — walk-out and defeat **reset the dungeon**, and the **escrow rule** holds everything gained inside until the guardian falls (payout on victory; forfeit on walk-out; forfeit plus normal loss consequences on defeat); entry screens telegraph all of it before the choice. Dungeons are **one-time** (cleared = ground). The **lair-dungeon class** converts the five tier-3 signatures' lairs into small procedural dungeons with R-card rewards — closing the banked lesser/greater-lairs thread (greater lairs are dungeons) and S18 concern 7 (the interior resident is a different register from the roaming one). **The five Mox dungeon laws (Chris-authored, symmetric-but-boss-favoring):** Caldera — both start with a Mountain; Reliquary — both start with a Plains; Deepwood — both start with a Llanowar Elves (the first pool card used as a modifier); Drowned Reach — both draw a bonus card; Barrowlands — both start with a 2/2 Zombie (the S17 token def). Every law is an existing Modifier-enum member; stronghold laws will be partisan and more powerful (S22 design).

*The Mox guardians (all Scryfall-verified by planner search except as flagged; implementer re-verification blocker-level):* Reya Dawnbringer (Pearl, Reliquary Wastes, **INV #33**), Arcanis the Omnipotent (Sapphire, Drowned Reach, **ONS #66**), Drana (Jet, Barrowlands, as pooled), Drakuseth, Maw of Flames (Ruby, Shattered Caldera, **CMM #535 — Chris corrected from #515**), Titania, Protector of Argoth (Emerald, Deepwood, **MH2 #416**). **Guardians are mono-color — the warp is reserved for the stronghold lords** (Chris-ratified): the elder powers keep orthodox single-flow magic; tri-color-on-duals identity belongs to the five bosses, making duals' first AI users the S22 stronghold decks. **Prize structure:** the Mox (fixed, `prizeOnly`) + the guardian's own card (the four new legendaries enter the pool at shopTier R) + one color-prize roll (R/T3 of the dungeon's color). Guardian decklists per dungeon-design §9 with **Chris's Reya swap** (Restoration Angel → Cunning Tactician; Resto's non-Angel restriction is nearly dead in an angel-heavy list). Titania's enablers: the Onslaught cycling-land cycle (Barren Moor ✔; mates ⚠) + Evolving Wilds (**BRC #184**, old frame), shopTier 1 with priceOverrides. Moxen ⚠ pending fetch.

*Lore canon (Chris-authored, recorded for the story bible):* the Cinquefoil ring order **WBRUG** is canonical — three of the plane's five adjacent mana flows (W–B, R–U, U–G) are enemy pairs elsewhere; the plane's mana runs in channels it shouldn't. The five stronghold lords ride these warped currents (triples **Wbg, Bwr, Rbu, Urg, Guw**) in their competition for dominion, and that competition is warping the plane toward the endgame threat. **Worldgen consequence:** spoke color order fixes to the WBRUG cyclic sequence (seed-jittered rotation and reflection preserve variety; adjacency is invariant) — rides S20. Blue's geometry (both flows warped) is noted for the Unwinder design. The legendary-reservation thread is resolved: **stronghold residents are custom legendaries** (S22 authoring, in progress planner-side); **real legendaries are dungeon guardians**; Baru remains a pool card and beast-deck lieutenant with no reserved role.
