# The mage cleansheet — Tier 2 (director round draft)

*Planner, 2026-09-05. Second section of the S29 design doc. Tier 2: journeyman, 10 life, anteCount 1, roam the approach. 40 cards, 17 lands: basics plus two of the pair's Ravnica dual (the shockland is the headline ante). Pool cards only; no gold cards; at most one tier-3 card per deck. Names and portraits retained; each mage is reassigned a flowing pair by the flavour of their name.*

## Rulings (Chris, 2026-09-05)
- Tier 2 = three mage-mage crosses and two mage-beast crosses. Tier 3 will use different pairings of both kinds.
- The five flowing pairs: WB, UR, BR, WG, UG (the still pairs belong to the petal courts).

## Mistress Vael — WB, "the Tithe-Reeve" (lifegain-drain)
```
8 Plains · 7 Swamp · 2 Godless Shrine
3 Soul Warden
2 Suntail Hawk
2 Youthful Valkyrie
2 Child of Night
2 Vampire Nighthawk
2 Blood Artist
2 Indulgent Aristocrat
2 Spirit Link
2 Unearth
1 Gravedigger
1 Swords to Plowshares
1 Pacifism
1 Wrath of God        ← the tier-3 card
```
40 / 17 / 23. Every creature entering gains, every creature dying drains; the Aristocrat turns chump blocks into Blood Artist pings and Unearth brings the pinger back. Wrath with a Blood Artist on the table is the deck's one big turn, and the AI needs to see it (a Wrath that drains for N is not a Wrath that costs N).
**Teaches:** the tier-1 lesson twice over — go around, and now also that a board wipe against this deck can lose you the game.

## Kessa Emberhand — UR, "the Stormcaller" (spells and tempo)
```
8 Mountain · 7 Island · 2 Steam Vents
3 Young Pyromancer
2 Arc Mage
2 Man-o'-War
1 Aether Channeler
3 Lightning Bolt
2 Shock
1 Blaze
1 Hordeling Outburst
2 Brainstorm
2 Boomerang
2 Essence Scatter
1 Counterspell
1 Air Elemental
```
40 / 17 / 23; fifteen instants and sorceries. The Sparkwright with the Bloom's bounce: every Boomerang is a tempo play *and* an Elemental. This is the deck Chris named when he pitched the Pyromancer.
**Teaches:** don't overcommit into open blue mana; the Pyromancer is still the engine.

## Adept Maelin — BR, "the Pyre-Warden" (goblin sacrifice)
```
8 Swamp · 7 Mountain · 2 Blood Crypt
2 Skirk Prospector
2 Raging Goblin
2 Goblin Piker
2 Hordeling Outburst
2 Goblin Grenade
2 Indulgent Aristocrat
2 Blood Artist
2 Unearth
2 Gravedigger
1 Gallows Djinn
1 Dark Ritual
1 Lightning Bolt
1 Terror
1 Siege-Gang Commander   ← the tier-3 card (⚠ confirm its tier)
```
40 / 17 / 23. The Warband's goblins as the Sexton's fodder: Outburst tokens are Goblins, so they feed the Grenade and the Prospector; Unearth returns a Piker or a Prospector; the Siege-Gang is four bodies and a sacrifice outlet in one card. Reach is Grenade to the face.
**Teaches:** the AI's creatures aren't there to attack — count the Grenades, not the goblins.

## Brennor of the Glade — WG, "the Sanctuary" (the protected healer)
```
9 Forest · 6 Plains · 2 Temple Garden
3 Gladecover Scout
2 Blurred Mongoose
2 Birds of Paradise
2 Soul Warden
2 Youthful Valkyrie
2 Rancor
2 Blanchwood Armor
2 Spirit Link
2 Giant Growth
1 Glorious Anthem
1 Swords to Plowshares
1 Pacifism
1 Restoration Angel
```
40 / 17 / 23. Temple Garden is a Forest, so the Armor counts eleven. Spirit Link on a Rancored, Armored Scout is a hexproof trampler that gains ten a hit. No tier-3 card; the deck doesn't need one.
**Teaches:** the Wardener's lesson (edicts, Wrath, deathtouch) now with a life total you also have to get through.

## Pell of the Shallows — UG, "the Tidesower" (landfall mill and ramp)
```
7 Island · 6 Forest · 2 Breeding Pool · 2 Evolving Wilds
4 Hedron Crab
2 Cathartic Adept
2 Traumatizer
3 Rampant Growth
2 Birds of Paradise
2 Blurred Mongoose
2 Timberland Guide
2 Brainstorm
2 Essence Scatter
1 Counterspell
1 Altar of Dementia
```
40 / 17 / 23. The Tidewright's crabs fed by the Wardener's ramp: Rampant Growth is three cards milled per Crab, the Wilds are six. Mongoose and Guide hold the ground while the library empties. The Crab's landfall on Rampant Growth and the Wilds is exactly the double-dip flagged when the word was chosen.
**Teaches:** ramp is landfall fuel; kill the Crabs *and* count the lands the AI has in hand.

## Tier-3 preview (for the next round, not for ratification now)
Same five pairs, the *other* parent lines, master, 12 life, anteCount 2, one or two tier-3 cards each, and the pair's ABU dual as the headline ante (R-tier). Candidate crosses:
- WB — Sexton × the Choir: reanimating angels (Zombify a Serra) — mage-beast
- UR — Tidewright × Sparkwright: mill-and-burn, Arc Mage discarding into self-mill — mage-mage
- BR — Sparkwright × the Specters: spells and discard — mage-beast
- WG — Wardener × the Pride: auras on a wide anthem board — mage-beast
- UG — Tidewright × the Wurms: mill until the Wurms arrive, Altar as the finisher — mage-beast
That's one mage-mage and four mage-beast; if you want the balance closer to tier 2's, UG can be Tidewright × Wardener's other half (Birds ramp into Formation) as mage-mage.
