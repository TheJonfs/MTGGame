## The implementer's first read for S47's scoping (from a survey of the editor, the Lab, the resolver and the save)

- **The match series — a wrapper, not a new controller.** `MatchController` (ui/play/match-controller.ts:284) runs ONE game: `start()` is memoized and the `Game` is built in the constructor, so game two is a new controller. Play/draw is already a rule (`rules.startingPlayer`). The world's own pattern owns a sequence of duels by `startX → match.start().then(finishX)` (world-controller.ts:1364/1388 and eight more pairs). **A best-of-three is the same pattern one level up**: a series object chaining three controllers (the loser of the last chooses play/draw), sideboarding a screen between them. The single match needs no change.
- **The background field — headless is already there.** The Lab's pool (ui/lab/lab-pool.ts) is Web Workers, `min(4, cores − 1)`, one job = one pairing × N games, ~7.5 games/s on six workers (implementer-notes). The worker only calls `runMatch` with `HeuristicAgent` — the same call `open:rr` and the sweeps make in Node. **A round's cost**: sixteen seats is eight best-of-threes (~20 games) — seconds; a hundred is fifty (~125 games) — ~20 s on four workers while the human plays. The open question is the workers' lifetime inside the world's screens, not the engine.
- **The ladder — the resolver fits.** `resolveMatchup(opponent, knobs, legacy, phase) → {life, profile, entrance, ante}` (world/matchup.ts:47) reads tier tables from the knobs; `convocationEntrance[round]` is the same `Record<index, …>` shape, and `legacyTerm` is a documented additive hook already. A round-indexed table is a knob plus one resolver branch.
- **The save — a second key, not the world save.** The world save is `world-save-v7` with a reserved `gauntlet` object that grows by optional fields; the precedent for state outside it is the profile's `shandalar-legacy` key. **An event in progress wants its own key** (the pool, the deck, the standings, the seed, the round) — a Convocation is not a place in the world, and resuming one must not touch the campaign's save.
- **The builders — the salvage assembler is too thin.** `assembleSalvageDeck` (world/salvage.ts:94) drops the two dearest per colour and fills basics by pips — no curve, no rating. `shopPrice` is mana value × tier, a rarity number, not a quality one. **A Limited builder needs a real card rating** (tier-and-price will over-rate expensive cards); the round-robin's key-piece rates and a per-card win contribution from the Lab are the first data for one.
- **The editor — reusable with a pool source.** The legality panel and the door picker already take a `Format` (`format:open` today; `checkDeck` with `restricted`/`banned`). The gaps for Limited: a `kind: "limited"`; the ownership check fed the sealed pool as its collection; and the editor's spares and save read `world.player.collection` / write `world.decks` — a tournament deck needs its own source and destination.
- **Pack recipes** are pure data over the pool's tiers (a `slots × weights` table and a seeded draw) — the smallest of the seven pieces.
