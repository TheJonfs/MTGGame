# Token art: weird_x_x_ur (Weird Token — Experimental Overload's)

Card-art class (the elemental-token precedent). The Weird grows from the ash of
spent spells — instants and sorceries given unstable form. Rendered 1:1,
center-cropped to the frame's 5:4 art window. S22 playtest r1.

## Visual descriptor (locked)

```text
An unstable elemental weird of fused magic: a roiling amorphous body
half crackling red flame and half flowing blue water-glass, the two
halves spiraling around each other without mixing, arcs of energy
snapping between them, a single bright core like a captive star, molten
droplets and steam curling away. Dark laboratory stone behind it lit by
its own light, no people. The seething figure fills the frame.
```
