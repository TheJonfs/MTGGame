## Concerns

1. **The `door.byRule` lines are keyed by FIELD but worded for one court.** "Bring bodies to the fire. **Twelve**, at the least." is now the line for *any* `minCreatures` door (the two S37/S38 controller tests, whose test gates ask 13 and 14 creatures, now hear "Twelve"). Either the lines lose their numbers, or the pack keys by site (`door.bySite`) with the field lines as fallback. One-line change either way; planner's call before S41 attaches the gates.
2. **Two courts' own lists fail their own gates** — by design (the gate is the intruder's), but worth a look: Odile's list has **11** creature cards (her gate asks the player for 12); Zinnia's carries Hedron Crabs and Walls (power < 2). Meliyan and Isaura keep theirs (the document says so).
3. **Shadow Summoning's first printing is The Lord of the Rings (LTR #226)**; the fetched scan is Tolkien's Army of the Dead. The name is generic, the art is not. Options: keep; pin TDC when it has a highres scan (it does not today); or our frame only. Chris's call.
4. **Odile at 20% against a finished deck, and the two engines that never fire** (the Reaper's harvest, Cairnbrand). The harvest's three-fifths rule is conservative and the list wins without it; if the planner wants the Reaper to *play* like the Reaper, loosen it to half (one constant in `floodSinkGated`) and re-run `pnpm flood-sim --only reaper`. Cairnbrand's seven-mana activation in a seventeen-land aggro list is simply rare.
5. **A named counter on a target is now "harmful"** in the AI's effect classification (so the Sphere never marks its own side). True for this pool (the accumulators are self-scoped); a future helpful named counter on a target (a shield counter) would need the classifier to look at what reads the counter.
6. **Two registered simplifications** (R-097): `sourcePower` reads zero once the source has left (Odile dying with her trigger on the stack deals nothing — CR would use LKI); `manaSpent` is the printed cost plus X (no spell cost modifiers exist; the line in `game.ts` says so).
7. **The ZONE_CHANGE event payload grew a field** (`powerBefore`, creatures leaving the battlefield only). Replays consume ACTION/RNG entries only, so old logs replay; anything that diffed EVENT streams byte-for-byte against a pre-S40 log would see it. Nothing in the repo does.
8. **`flood-decks.ts` is generated from the document's code blocks** (the generator lived in the session's scratchpad). S41 should make the catalog the home and delete the sim copy, or the lists will drift from the document the way hand-mirrors always have.
9. **The legends' "price convention"**: every prizeOnly def in the pool carries no `shopTier` and no `priceOverride`, the phase-one lords and ministers included — there was no price to set. If the brief meant a sale value, that is a new field.
