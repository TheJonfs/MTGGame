# The Bolt probe — 57 of 200 Sealed pools rolled a Lightning Bolt and were built with it played; 2280 games an arm

## The base arm (the pilot as it is)

- Games with a Bolt in hand at some point: 1198 of 2280. The deck won 47.2% of them, and 45.1% of the games without one.
- Bolts cast: 1141 — 1058 at a creature (mean power 1.9), 83 at the face (their life then: mean 4.7), 0 at our own side.
- **Turns a kill was on offer and the Bolt stayed in hand**: 1080, in 737 games (61.5% of the games that saw a Bolt). Mean 1.5 such turns in those games.
- What was answering: the scorer 1028, burnhold (book 160) 52.
- The board at a hold: the creature the Bolt would kill had mean power 2.0; their board's power 5.6; our life 17.7; 96.6% in our own turn.

## The eager arm (a pass with a kill on offer becomes the Bolt, power two or more)

- Paired over 2280 games: base 46.2%, eager 46.4%, **+0.2 ± 0.7**.
- The override fired in 554 games (575 Bolts). In those games: base 49.5%, eager 50.4%, +0.9 ± 2.9.
- The forced Bolts hit creatures of mean power 2.5.
