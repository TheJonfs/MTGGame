## 1. The Intake (W) — the Warden's law, read again

**The law:** creatures the intruder controls enter the battlefield tapped.
**Phase one's readings:** the Warden (W·bg) — untapping costs (the prison); Seraphina (BG) — being tapped is fatal (the execution).
**Phase two's reading:** what arrives is undone (tempo), and what stays tapped burns.

### The Bailiff — lord, W·ur (Jeskai) ✅
> **The Bailiff** — {1}{W}{W}{U}{R} Legendary Creature — Angel, 3/4
> Flying, vigilance, haste
> Whenever The Bailiff attacks, you may return target tapped creature to its owner's hand.

*Lightning Angel's descendant. Haste narrows the intruder's window to instant speed: the bounce happens the turn he lands. The "may" spares an attack alongside untapped blockers and opens the self-bounce of an ETB creature. Counterplay the pair teaches: flash bodies (the Escort, Restoration Angel) untap before he attacks; non-creature threats are never bounced; tokens bounced are tokens lost; Swords, or a four-toughness flash blocker, at instant speed. Bill: zero words.*

### Odile, the Tallyflame — court, UR ✅
> **Odile, the Tallyflame** — {2}{U}{R} Legendary Creature — Elemental Shaman, 1/4
> Whenever you draw a card, Odile, the Tallyflame deals damage equal to its power to each tapped creature.

*Under the Intake the intruder's arrivals are tapped at the court's draw step; so are last turn's attackers. One damage a turn to everything the intruder played or swung with; X/1s die on arrival; attacking without vigilance costs a point per attacker. Scales with extra draws (Brainstorm is three triggers) or a pump. The controller's own creatures are untapped at their draw step, so the "burns your own" cost is a discipline about instant-speed draws after tapping, not a symmetric tax — say so in the flavour, not the text. The court's shape gate should be a creature floor: bring bodies to the fire. Bill: zero — the DRAW collector exists (the Bloom Gauntlet's WU Soldier); Pyroclasm's each-creature with the tapped predicate; the Altar's power reference.*

### Static Sphere — UW ✅
> **Static Sphere** — {1}{U}{W} Artifact
> When Static Sphere enters, put a static counter on target permanent.
> At the beginning of each upkeep, tap each permanent with a static counter on it.

*The counter outlives the Sphere: a second Sphere, or a Restoration Angel blinking the first, re-arms every mark. Disenchant answers the Sphere, not the mark. The upkeep tap lands before the draw step — a marked creature is tapped for Odile's fire and for the Bailiff's bounce (which clears the counter: the intruder's one clean out, at the cost of recasting under the Intake). A marked land can be tapped for mana in response to the trigger: a timing tax, not a lock; leave it. Bill: Clio's counter word; a filtered mass tap; an every-upkeep trigger (half a word if the Season's is controller-only).*

### Sacred Helix — RW ✅
> **Sacred Helix** — {X}{R}{R}{W}{W} Instant
> Sacred Helix deals damage equal to the amount of mana spent to cast it to any target. You gain that much life.

*Four for four-and-four at instant speed, one more per mana above; a Helix that becomes a finisher. The lord's instant-speed four-damage answer, and the player's R-tier gold hunt. Bill: a spell-cost total on the stack item ("mana spent"), to be reused by a second card. Note for the pool: Char (⚠ {2}{R} instant, 4 to any target, 2 to you) as the mono-red answer the player who arrives without the gold still needs — a pool add, separate from this pair.*

**Seats:** ⬜ the WUR stronghold, the UR court. **Gate:** stronghold — within WUR; court — see §6.

## 2. The Tithe (B) — the Usher's law, read again

**The law:** whenever a creature dies, the intruder loses 1 life.
**Phase one's readings:** the Usher (B·rw) — deaths are drained (the aristocrat).
**Phase two's reading:** deaths are a *resource* — the graveyard is the lord's larder; what the Tithe kills, the lord keeps.

### The Reeve — lord, B·ug (Sultai) ✅
> **The Reeve** — {1}{B}{B}{U}{G} Legendary Creature — Nightmare Warlock, 3/5
> Deathtouch
> {1}{U}: Target player mills three cards.
> {3}{B}{G}: Return target creature card from a graveyard to the battlefield.

*Two abilities that are one plan across three colours: mill (either player — his own library for targets, the intruder's for loot) into reanimation from *a* graveyard. Deathtouch and the deck carry the removal; every kill is the Tithe's trigger and, a turn later, his. The Usher does things on entry; the Reeve does things every turn — the other axis. Bill: mill and the Zombify word with an any-graveyard scope (Buried Alive's "a graveyard" direction, inverted); deathtouch exists. Zero to a quarter word.*

### Zinnia, the Undertow — court, UG ✅
> **Zinnia, the Undertow** — {2}{U}{G} Legendary Creature — Merfolk Druid, 2/4
> Whenever a creature an opponent controls leaves the battlefield, that player mills cards equal to its power.

*The leaving creature's power, so she scales with the opponent's deck: a chump mills one, a Wurm bounced mills seven, a sweep is a library. "Leaves" puts Man-o'-War, Boomerang, the Channeler, Prey Upon and the Reeve's deathtouch on one plan — tempo becomes mill without black. Bill: a leaves-the-battlefield collector (dies exists; the wider event is the half-word) with last-known-information power (the Altar's word).*

### Glimpse the Unthinkable — UB ✅ (⚠ verify: {U}{B} Sorcery — Target player mills ten cards. Ravnica)
*The R-tier version of the Crab's plan: a library in one card. Feeds the Reeve's larder from either side.*

### Putrefy — BG ✅ (⚠ verify: {1}{B}{G} Instant — Destroy target artifact or creature. It can't be regenerated. Ravnica)
*The deck's clean kill, and for the player the first gold that answers a law and a lord's body with one card.*

**Seats:** ⬜ the BUG stronghold, the UG court. **Gate:** stronghold — within BUG; court — see §6.

## 3. The Toll (R) — the Stoker's law, read again

**The law:** whenever the intruder casts a spell, the Toll deals 1 damage to them.
**Phase one's reading:** the Stoker (R·ub) — cast more than they can afford to answer (the Phoenix loops, the Weird on the ash).
**Phase two's reading:** every noncreature wound is the lord's meal; and the law is an enchantment on the table.

### The Fordkeeper — lord, R·wg (Naya) ✅
> **The Fordkeeper** — {1}{R}{R}{W}{G} Legendary Creature — Spirit Warrior, 4/5
> Whenever a noncreature source you control deals damage, you gain that much life.
> Lands you control have "{2}, {T}: This land deals 1 damage to any target."

*Tamanoa's descendant. The law is a noncreature source he controls, so the Toll's tax is his lifegain (⚠ implementer: confirm the law permanent is controlled by the lord). Every burn spell in the deck is a Helix under him — Bolt three and three, Pyroclasm once per creature, Sacred Helix twice — and the lands are the floor when the hand is empty: eight lands at {2},{T} is two or three pings a turn, pressure rather than a lock. Removable only through him (Vindicate aside). The name is the flood's: roads become fords, and the ford is where the toll is taken. Bill: the class-grant on lands (Frondland Felidar's word with a land filter); the lifegain on a DEALS_DAMAGE collector with the S31 `type` filter negated to noncreature. Half a word at most.*

### Ovna, the Enchantress — court, WG ✅
> **Ovna, the Enchantress** — {2}{G}{W} Legendary Creature — Nymph, 2/2
> Ovna, the Enchantress gets +1/+1 for each enchantment you control.
> Whenever you cast an enchantment spell, draw a card.

*The Toll is an artifact enchantment the court controls, so she is a 3/3 the moment she lands, and the pool's green-white enchantments — Pacifism, Glare of Subdual, Blanchwood Armor, Rancor, Spirit Link — are her deck: control and buffs that each grow her and replace themselves. An attacker that wants the table full. (The Pridemate shape was considered and set aside: the lifegain is the Fordkeeper's, not the law's, and the court is fought without him.) Bill: zero — Blanchwood's count with an enchantment filter; the Pyromancer's cast trigger with an enchantment filter.*

### Powerstone Minefield — RW ✅ (⚠ verify: {2}{R}{W} Enchantment — Whenever a creature attacks or blocks, Powerstone Minefield deals 2 damage to it. Apocalypse)
*A controlling Naya card: every attack or block costs two, from a noncreature source — the Fordkeeper gains two a body — and it is an enchantment for Ovna's count.*

### Savage Twister — RG ✅ (⚠ verify: {X}{R}{G} Sorcery — Savage Twister deals X damage to each creature. Mirage)
*The swing: a large noncreature source on every creature at once; under the Fordkeeper, X life per creature hit. The deck's reset and its heal in one card.*

**Seats:** ⬜ the RWG stronghold, the WG court. **Gate:** stronghold — within RWG; court — see §6.

## 4. The Risen Tide (U) — the Unwinder's law, read again

**The law:** the controller may play an additional land on each of their turns.
**Phase one's reading:** the Unwinder (U·rg) — replay what you bounce; the land engine runs at zero tempo (tempo).
**Phase two's reading:** spend what you don't need — the spare land is a spell back, or mana for a sink (attrition).

### The Dredger — lord, U·bw (Esper) ✅
> **The Dredger** — {1}{U}{U}{W}{B} Legendary Creature — Sphinx, 3/4
> Flying
> {W}{U}{B}, Sacrifice a land: Return target instant or sorcery card from your graveyard to your hand.

*The Unwinder replays lands; the Dredger spends them — two drops a turn means a spare land every turn, and a spare land is a Wrath, a Swords or a counterspell back. A control lord whose body blocks and whose rebuy is the clock. Bill: zero — sacrifice-a-land as a cost (the Lumberjack's word without the subtype); Gravedigger's return-to-hand with an instant-or-sorcery predicate.*

### Isaura, the Levy — court, BW ✅
> **Isaura, the Levy** — {2}{W}{B} Legendary Creature — Human Cleric, 2/3
> {1}{B}: Target creature gets −1/−1 until end of turn.
> {2}{W}{W}: Put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control.

*The law's benefit made legible: nothing triggers on the drop; the court simply reaches four mana on turn three and has a sink every turn after. Repeatable death for one-toughness creatures (the salvage pack is full of them — the answer is toughness and tokens, not a card) and a team that grows every turn from four. The deck runs land-heavy on purpose: the black and white cycling lands four each, Bitterblossom and Raise the Alarm for the bodies. A levee holds the water; a levy raises the army. Bill: a negative pump (Giant Growth's word, negative — trivial if not already allowed); the Season's scoped team-counters.*

### Undermine — UB ✅ (⚠ verify: {U}{U}{B} Instant — Counter target spell. Its controller loses 3 life. Invasion)
*The deck's win condition made of answers: rebought by the Dredger every turn, the intruder either casts into it and loses three or doesn't cast and waits.*

### Absorb — WU ✅ (⚠ verify: {W}{U}{U} Instant — Counter target spell. You gain 3 life. Invasion)
*The mirror: the counter that heals; the same rebuy.*

**Seats:** ⬜ the UBW stronghold, the BW court. **Gate:** stronghold — within UBW; court — see §6.

## 5. The Season (G) — the Sower's law, read again

**The law:** at the beginning of the controller's upkeep, put a +1/+1 counter on each creature they control.
**Phase one's reading:** the Sower (G·wu) — a swarm that compounds (the Formation's faeries under the Season).
**Phase two's reading:** growth spent as violence — the counters are the magazine; the upkeep reloads it.

### The Reaper — lord, G·br (Jund) ✅
> **The Reaper** — {1}{G}{G}{B}{R} Legendary Creature — Snake, 4/4
> At the beginning of each upkeep, create a 1/1 green Snake creature token.
> {B}{R}{G}, Sacrifice a creature: Creatures you control get +X/+0 and gain haste and lifelink until end of turn, where X is the sacrificed creature's power.

*The Sower sows; the Reaper harvests. A snake every upkeep — the intruder's too, so a fresh blocker stands on their turn — and the Season grows the whole line on his. Sacrifice the oldest snake for the alpha, the freshest for haste and lifelink at +1; the choice is the play. No trample (chump-blocking must keep working against a wide board); if the seeds show stalls, menace on the grant is the Jund answer. Bill: a token-each-upkeep trigger (Bitterblossom's shape at the every-upkeep timing Static Sphere also needs — one word between them); the sacrifice cost with the Altar's power reference; team pump and the S32 keyword grant at team scope; a 1/1 green Snake token def. A quarter word.*

### Meliyan, the Torment — court, BR ✅
> **Meliyan, the Torment** — {2}{B}{R} Legendary Creature — Devil, 2/3
> Whenever another creature you control dies, Meliyan, the Torment deals damage equal to that creature's power to target opponent.

*The death side of the pair: under the Season every creature dies larger than it was cast, so a chump block is a burn spell and the Reaper's sacrifices hit twice. Chris's note, recorded: a harder-hitting aristocrat payoff than Blood Artist or the Usher when the bodies are big — fine, because she is a 2/3 and the big body has to die first. The damage is hers, not the dying creature's (LKI power on a DIES collector — the Altar's word; the Usher's trigger with a power amount). Bill: zero.*

### Poison-Tip Archer — BG ✅ (⚠ verify: {2}{B}{G} Creature — Elf Archer, 2/3, reach, deathtouch. Whenever another creature dies, each opponent loses 1 life. M19)
*Chosen over Spiritmonger to avoid installing regeneration. A deterrent while the snakes gather (reach and deathtouch), and a second death payoff that fires on the Reaper's sacrifices and the intruder's deaths alike.*

### Voracious Cobra — RG ✅ (⚠ verify: {2}{R}{G} Creature — Snake, 2/2, first strike. Whenever Voracious Cobra blocks or becomes blocked by a creature, destroy that creature. Invasion)
*A snake among snakes; a wall the intruder cannot attack through and a body they cannot block. Invasion keeps paying the project's bills.*

**Seats:** ⬜ the GBR stronghold, the BR court. **Gate:** stronghold — within GBR; court — see §6.

## 6. The High Grounds — the courts' prizes, their entrances, and their gates ✅

**The idea (Chris, 2026-09-18):** the five courts guard the five places that stayed dry — legendary dual lands, one per flowing pair, each carrying a repeatable ability in the court's own identity. Mox-class by impact rather than shape: no card slot, no mana to deploy, removable only by Vindicate, an effect every turn for the rest of the game. **Each court fights on its own ground**: the court's entrance puts its High Ground on the battlefield, one-sided (the Heart's roots path). Win the court and it is yours. prizeOnly; one each; never in a shop.

| court | the High Ground | text | under the court | the gate to fight her |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Odile (UR) | **Tallyflame Court** — Legendary Land | {T}: Add {U} or {R}. {2}{U}{R}, {T}: Draw a card. | every draw is Odile's fire | **at least 12 creature cards** |
| Zinnia (UG) | **Wrackroot** — Legendary Land | {T}: Add {U} or {G}. {3}{U}{G}, {T}: Put target creature on top of its owner's library. | the creature leaves before it lands on top: Zinnia mills it — repeatable removal | **no creature cards with power less than 2** (no Crabs, dorks, Artists, walls) |
| Ovna (WG) | **Shevelport** — Legendary Land | {T}: Add {W} or {G}. {2}{G}{W}, {T}: Return target artifact, enchantment, or land card from your graveyard to your hand. | Ovna's engine; re-arms Static Sphere; gives back drowned land | **no instant cards** (no blowout in response to an aura; sorcery-speed removal stays; flash creatures are creatures) |
| Isaura (WB) | **Obsidian Observatory** — Legendary Land | {T}: Add {W} or {B}. {2}{W}{B}, {T}: Creatures you control gain vigilance and menace until end of turn. | the alpha for Isaura's army | **at least half the deck is land cards** |
| Meliyan (BR) | **Cairnbrand** — Legendary Land | {T}: Add {B} or {R}. {4}{B}{R}, {T}, Sacrifice a creature: Return target creature card from your graveyard to the battlefield. | the sacrifice is Meliyan's burn | **no cards with mana value greater than 4** (every carried lord is out) |

*Five gates of five kinds: a type floor, a stat floor, a type ban, a fraction, a curve ceiling. Three ride S37's `deckRule` fields (`minCreatures`, `maxManaValue`, a type ban is `bannedTypes`); two are new — a stat predicate on creatures (`minCreaturePower`) and a fraction (`minLandFraction`). The rule engine grows, which Chris wants for later challenges. Bills for the lands: Library's shape (mana + a second tap ability); Temporal Spring's put-on-top; a typed graveyard-to-hand; the S32 team keyword grant (menace exists — Boggart Brute); Zombify with the Altar's sacrifice cost. Zero words beyond the two rule fields.*

**The deep water's prize** (the phase-two Heart): open — the one place a singleton of the Power's blue trio could sit, the way the Lotus sits behind the reflection. Not decided.
