# The Convocation — the Open format's seed lists (draft 2 — Chris's feedback of 2026-09-30 applied; eleven lists)

*Planner, 2026-09-30. Eleven sixty-card lists — the ten lines the pool supports plus Chris's Goblins —, for the select-and-repair library. Built to the Open's rules (`convocation-formats-draft-2.md` §1.1): 60 minimum, 4-of, basics uncapped, the restricted list at one (Black Lotus, the five Moxen, Time Walk, the five High Grounds, Demonic Tutor, Library of Alexandria), the laws banned, the legends at four, prizeOnly legal. Every list is untested — these are the seeds a team brings to a tournament before the Lab has played a game; Claude Code tunes them in the Lab as they land. Restricted cards are marked (r). Pool: 233.*

**A note on the Open's texture.** With Black Lotus and the on-colour Moxen at one each, every competitive list runs them, and the format's turn one is Vintage's. That is the format working as designed (§1.1: the mana is the texture, power is what's restricted); the Pauper and Peasant formats exist for the game without it.

## What to test first (Claude Code, the Lab)
A round-robin of the eleven at 100 games both seats, master both. "Solvable" is one list over 65% against the field; the suspects are Sparks (the burn) and Mardu (the drain); the Muster against the Warband is the first pairing (Chris's question). The read wanted: the win-rate table, the three-clock mill's share by library, and which restricted cards each list actually casts (the Lotus and the Moxen should cast in every list that runs them; a list that never casts its High Ground should drop it).
