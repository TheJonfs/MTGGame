# The mage inversion — the tier-2/3 mages in the flood (planner's draft, 2026-09-21)

*The design (ADR-122, §9): in phase two the ten tier-2/3 mages take the five **still** pairs — the pairs phase one's courts held — so the world's roaming multicolour opponents mirror phase one's exactly. Names, portraits and epithets kept; each mage keeps its primary colour and turns the other. Tier 1 unchanged. Forty cards, seventeen lands (basics, the pair's shockland, the pair's ABU dual at tier 3), no gold cards, no prizeOnly, at most two tier-3 titles at tier 3, one at tier 2 — the S29 rules. The phase-two pool (227) is the pool: this is where the Lumberjack finally lives.*
