## Suggested next — S59, the campaign linkage, as it would be built
The S54 read still holds; this is it against the code as it stands.

**1. The Convocation never writes the world save; it posts to an outbox.**
- The journey rewrites its whole save on every autosave, so a second writer would be overwritten.
- The Convocation appends to its own key: `{ id, kind: "kept" | "prize" | "spent", cards?, gold?, invitationId? }`.
- The world drains the outbox on load and on focus, applies each id once (a new provenance source, `"convocation"`), and deletes an entry only after its own save succeeds.
- About 150 lines with tests. This is the piece to build first; everything else rides it.

**2. Entry is an invitation, bought or earned in the journey.**
- Gold cannot be taken from the Convocation's page (the same race), so the cost is paid in the world and held as a small additive save field.
- The door reads unspent invitations (read-only, as it reads decks today). Entering posts "spent"; the ledger records the id so a reload cannot spend it twice.
- An invitation can carry the shape: a single event, the short Convocation, the four days.
- **The question to rule on first:** how one is earned. A town board at a price, a quest reward, or both. This decides where in the world's content it lands.

**3. Fence (a), as ruled: kept cards exclude the power slot.**
- The packs' power slot holds the Lotus, the five Moxen, Time Walk, the five High Grounds, the Manafleur and the Cinquefont. The Vault counts Moxen held; the Manafleur is the Heart's prize.
- One filter at the finish: a kept card is never one of those fourteen. The trophy room already records kept cards; the filter is applied where the player chooses them.

**4. The prize table.**
- Gold or a card by place band, as a knob table like the campaign's other rewards, riding the same outbox.
- Needs the planner's numbers: the bands (champion, the eight, the top quarter) and what each pays.

**5. What it costs.** About one session: the outbox and its drain, the save field, the provenance source, the door's invitation chooser, the kept-card filter and the prize table. The fence is ruled; the earning of an invitation and the prize numbers are the two things I need from the planner.

**Also open:** Part 2's report when the games exist; the Sweep's fifteen; a re-authored pair or two for the Locks' guide.
