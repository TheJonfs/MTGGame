# The Bolt probe — 4 of 200 Sealed decks play Lightning Bolt; 160 games an arm

## The base arm (the pilot as it is)

- Games with a Bolt in hand at some point: 86 of 160. The deck won 51.2% of them, and 41.9% of the games without one.
- Bolts cast: 74 — 68 at a creature (mean power 1.8), 6 at the face (their life then: mean 3.5), 0 at our own side.
- **Turns a kill was on offer and the Bolt stayed in hand**: 87, in 56 games (65.1% of the games that saw a Bolt). Mean 1.6 such turns in those games.
- What was answering: the scorer 85, burnhold (book 160) 2.
- The board at a hold: the creature the Bolt would kill had mean power 1.8; their board's power 5.1; our life 17.5; 97.7% in our own turn.

| 3 | 10 | 80.0% |

- **Lost with a Bolt still in hand**: 5 of 85 losses (5.9%). In 2 of those a kill on a creature of power two or more had been on offer.

## The eager arm (a pass with a kill on offer becomes the Bolt, power two or more)

- Paired over 160 games: base 46.9%, eager 48.1%, **+1.3 ± 3.0**.
- The override fired in 42 games (42 Bolts). In those games: base 45.2%, eager 50.0%, +4.8 ± 11.5.
- The forced Bolts hit creatures of mean power 2.3.
