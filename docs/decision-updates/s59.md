## Parts 1–5 — the linkage (ADR-172)

- **The outbox is one storage key per entry** (`convocation-outbox:<id>`), not one key holding a list. *Deviation, see the handoff.* A post is one write; a delete is one removal; neither side ever rewrites the other's work.
- **An entry names its invitation, and the invitation names its journey** (`<seed>-p<phase>-s<sitting>`). A journey applies only its own; a phase-one and a phase-two save in one browser each take theirs.
- **Applied once:** the world keeps the ids it has applied (`convocation.applied`), applies an entry in memory inside its autosave, and deletes the entry only after the write has succeeded. A page closed between the write and the delete finds the id applied on its next load and only deletes.
- **The clock rides the autosave**: every save asks whether a sitting has come (`floor(steps ÷ convocationInterval)`), posts the invitation, and drops the unspent one before it. Also on load and on the page's focus.
- **`invitation.source`** is `clock | board | quest | grant`; only `clock` is written. Nothing else of the other routes is built.
- **Origin**: every new event is stamped `origin: "menu"` or `"journey"` (with `invitationId`). Only a journey event posts. `menuUnlocks` exists on the event and nothing writes it.
- **A single-shape invitation is a draft** (thirty-two seats, five rounds, the Umbel). *Mine: the brief names the shape, not the event. A Constructed single's "keep a card from your pool" would be the whole format's pool.*
- **The prize**: `convocationPrizeGold` {champion 300, eight 150, quarter 75, rest 25}, `convocationPrizeHard` 1.5, `convocationPrizeSingle` 0.5. The kept cards post home for the champion and the eight only (as the table has it), one entry a card.
- **The fence**: `keepable` — never one of the packs' power slot (`sets.json` → `power`, the fourteen; the world's side carries the same list, pinned to it by a test). Applied at the choice (the prize screen does not offer them; the controller refuses them) and again at the drain.
- **Text**: the brief's three lines as written. One line is mine and wants Chris's pen: what the journey says when the outbox is drained — *"From the Convocation: 150 gold, Serra Angel."*
