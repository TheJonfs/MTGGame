# The flood's seats — text (planner, for Chris's pen)

*2026-09-20. For `quests.json` under a `flood.seats` pack: for each site a `telegraph` (the approach), a `parley` (the seat's greeting at the door), a `door` (the refusal, already keyed by site — repeated here for the pack), a `fall` (the Chronicle's line), and a `prize` (the treasure line). The `{}` fields are the world's. Register as the phase-one packs: short sentences, the seat speaks in the third person of itself except where marked.*

---

## The strongholds

### Tidelock Weir — the Bailiff (the Intake)
- **telegraph**: *The road ends at a wall of water held back by stone. Nothing on the far side is moving. A gate stands in the weir, and the gate is open, which is worse.*
- **parley**: *"Property of the Weir," says the Bailiff, and does not say whose. "You may bring it in. You may not keep it long."*
- **door** (colour): *The gate knows your colours. It will not open to these.*
- **fall**: *The Weir is breached. The Bailiff's seizures are returned to the water, and the road runs through the stone.*
- **prize**: *The Bailiff's writ, and the lands of the Weir.*

### Marrowfen — the Reeve (the Tithe)
- **telegraph**: *The bog is quiet in the way a full larder is quiet. Things are kept here. The path is a causeway of what the water took, packed down and walked on.*
- **parley**: *"Everything that dies here is entered," says the Reeve. "Everything entered is mine to spend. You are not yet entered."*
- **door** (colour): *The gate knows your colours. It will not open to these.*
- **fall**: *Marrowfen is emptied. The Reeve's ledger closes on its last entry, and the bog gives back what it kept.*
- **prize**: *The Reeve's ledger, and the lands of the fen.*

### Emberford — the Fordkeeper (the Toll)
- **telegraph**: *The water is shallow here, and warm, and wrong. Someone has been burning the river to keep the crossing. The ford is lit from underneath.*
- **parley**: *"The toll is what you bring," says the Fordkeeper. "The river keeps the rest. Cross if you are certain."*
- **door** (colour): *The gate knows your colours. It will not open to these.*
- **fall**: *The ford goes dark. The Fordkeeper's fires drown, and the crossing is a crossing again.*
- **prize**: *The Fordkeeper's brand, and the lands of the ford.*

### Lockmere — the Dredger (the Risen Tide)
- **telegraph**: *A valley became a lake, and someone built locks across it. The water is raised in steps. At the top step the lake is deeper than the valley was.*
- **parley**: *"Nothing is lost in a flood," says the Dredger. "Only sunk. I bring things up. I can bring them up again."*
- **door** (colour): *The gate knows your colours. It will not open to these.*
- **fall**: *The locks open. What the Dredger raised runs down the steps and out, and the lake is a valley with water in it.*
- **prize**: *The Dredger's charter, and the lands of the mere.*

### Harrowmoor — the Reaper (the Season)
- **telegraph**: *The moor is furrowed like a field, and something is growing in the furrows. It is not grain. The rows are longer each time you look.*
- **parley**: *"The Sower sowed," says the Reaper. "I was what came up. Stand still, if you want to be counted with the harvest."*
- **door** (colour): *The gate knows your colours. It will not open to these.*
- **fall**: *The furrows are cut. The Reaper's rows lie flat on the moor, and nothing comes up in them.*
- **prize**: *The Reaper's scythe, and the lands of the moor.*

## The courts (on the High Grounds, in the deep water)

### Tallyflame Court — Odile (the Intake)
- **telegraph**: *An island in the deep water, and on it a court lit by a flame that does not warm. The flame counts. You can see it counting.*
- **parley**: *"Every card you turn, I turn to fire," says Odile. "Bring your creatures. Bring many. The tally is kinder to the many."*
- **door**: *Bring bodies to the fire. Twelve, at the least.*
- **fall**: *The Tallyflame gutters. Odile's count ends, and the court is a hall on a hill with a hearth in it.*
- **prize**: *Tallyflame Court — the ground itself, dry — and Odile's tally.*

### The Wrackroot Shallows — Zinnia (the Tithe)
- **telegraph**: *Roots break the surface of the deep water like the ribs of something drowned. The shallows between them are not shallow. Something moves under the tangle.*
- **parley**: *"What leaves you, the water keeps," says Zinnia. "The small things it takes first. Bring nothing small."*
- **door**: *Nothing small. The water takes the small things first.*
- **fall**: *The undertow slackens. Zinnia's roots settle, and the shallows are shallow.*
- **prize**: *Wrackroot — the ground itself, dry — and Zinnia's tangle.*

### Shevelport Green — Ovna (the Toll)
- **telegraph**: *One meadow above the flood, green as it was before, and greener. The court is a ring of standing things — stones, or something planted to look like stones. Nothing on the Green is sudden.*
- **parley**: *"Bring what lasts," says Ovna. "Set it down and leave it. The Green does not answer in the moment, and neither will you."*
- **door**: *No sudden things. The Green does not answer in the moment.*
- **fall**: *The Green goes fallow. Ovna's ring stands empty, and the meadow is a meadow.*
- **prize**: *Shevelport — the ground itself, dry — and Ovna's wreath.*

### The Obsidian Observatory — Isaura (the Risen Tide)
- **telegraph**: *A black tower on black rock, and from its top the whole flood is visible: where it rose, where it stopped, where it is rising still. Isaura has counted the ground that is left. She has counted yours.*
- **parley**: *"Half of what you bring must be ground," says Isaura. "The rest can be yours. The tower will see how much of yours there is."*
- **door**: *Half of what you bring must be ground. The rest can be yours.*
- **fall**: *The Observatory goes blind. Isaura's levy is disbanded, and the tower is a tower.*
- **prize**: *The Obsidian Observatory — the ground itself, dry — and Isaura's glass.*

### Cairnbrand Pyre — Meliyan (the Season)
- **telegraph**: *A cairn on the last dry rock, and on the cairn a fire that is fed with something. The smoke goes up and does not disperse. It is watching the water.*
- **parley**: *"Nothing dear," says Meliyan. "What you bring here, you will lose, and I will feel it go. Bring cheap things. Bring them in numbers."*
- **door**: *Nothing dear. What you bring here, you will lose.*
- **fall**: *The pyre goes out. Meliyan's smoke disperses, and the cairn is a cairn.*
- **prize**: *Cairnbrand — the ground itself, dry — and Meliyan's ember.*

## The Calyx and the deep water
- **the fords** (a ferry or ford tile's line, on first crossing): *The water is deep here, and someone has laid a way across it — stones, or a boat that knows the way. It leads to dry ground.*
- **the deep water** (the centre, before the lords fall — the S39 line stands): *The water is deep here.*
- **the fifth lord falls** (the Heart opens): *The fifth stronghold falls, and the deep water moves. Something under it has felt the last dam go. The way down is open.*
- **Chronicle, the fifth lord**: *Five holds broken in the flood. The Calyx is open.*
