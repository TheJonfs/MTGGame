## Part 3 — the Larder's plan

- **The vocabulary needed two fields:** `goal: "once"` and `pieces` (the cards the plan is content to return; the first is `piece`). With `once`, one piece in the graveyard is set up, a start beside it is the plan assembled, a second setup is refused, and the start may be aimed at any piece. Nothing changes for a `loop` plan (the Pall plays as before).
- **A seed list's plan** lives in `open-contributed.json` under `plans`; `open:gen` makes the list a combo list.
- **The plan as authored:** pieces Artisan of Kozilek and Angel of the Ruins; setup Entomb and Buried Alive (the Artisan, the Angel, a Pelakka Wurm); start Zombify and Graceful Restoration; dig Demonic Tutor; fuel Dark Ritual, the Lotus and four Moxen; answers: a counter on the start, graveyard exile.
- **Not in the plan:** the Angel's plainscycling as a setup (the S36 rule still cycles her for a reanimator in hand); the Artisan's own cast as a start (nine mana is ordinary play); "a bounce for the Artisan" as an answer (the plan has no such field; the AI's bounce already prefers the largest body).

## Part 4 — a better out-rule: built, measured, left off

On the revised sixteen (`open:rr --sideboarded-one`, 450 games a list, paired with a plain run):

| what leaves | mean gain from sideboarding |
|---|---|
| the lowest-rated card (as S56 shipped) | **+2.06** |
| dead cards first | +2.08 |
| four-ofs kept (mine) | +1.01 |
| dead first, four-ofs kept | +1.00 |
| all, with rule 9 (dead cards out for the best left) | +1.08 |

- **Dead-first almost never fires:** only the Locks hold fewer than eight creatures.
- **Keeping four-ofs is worse:** a four-of is often exactly what a matchup does not want (the Coin −6, the Ford −6, the Sweep −3 against the rules as they are).
- All three parts stay in `sideboard-ai.ts` behind `outRule`, off, with a test.
- **The mean fell from +3.4 to +2.1 with the revision**, and the Locks are why: they now lose 6.9 ± 5.3 points by sideboarding. Their fifteen's removal comes in for their counterspells. Keeping the counters in was worse still (−14.1): then the Dredgers and Serras leave.
