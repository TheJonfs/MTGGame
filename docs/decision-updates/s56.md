## Where the brief's design was not what the measurement supported

Each of these is a deviation in the handoff. The planner should rule on them.

1. **The counter rule (Part 1).** The brief's premise was that the pilot declines to counter, or sits on mana. The probe (`pnpm counter-probe`) says otherwise:
   - The Locks draw 5.2 counterspells a game and cast 3.1 (not "three of twelve").
   - Of the windows where a counter was in hand and an opposing spell on the stack, the Locks passed with the mana in 0.7 a game, on spells of mean mana value 1.2 (a Lotus, a Bolt, a Soul Warden).
   - The windows lost were lost to: **the colours not being on the battlefield at all** (about one a game for the Locks: Absorb is WUU and Undermine UUB in a three-colour deck); too few lands (0.9 a game); mana spent on its own turn (the Dredger, Serra, Vindicate, Tidewall); a counter already cast that turn.
   - Their end step with a counter held and another spell castable happens 0.2–0.4 times a game, and the castable spell is removal with nothing worth aiming it at.
   - **So the brief's two clauses were not built** (spend held mana at the end step; cast when the spell's worth exceeds the counter's held value). What was built instead is below.
2. **Dig harder (Part 3) was measured and not taken.** The brief's rule cost the Pall seven points.
3. **Rule 6 (creature counters) does not run against a plan that counterspells answer.** The Pall holds twenty creatures, and its setup and start are sorceries.
4. **Against a plan, graveyard exile takes the walls out first.** Not in the brief; it is what the brief's check (the Kiln against the Pall) needed.

## The counter rule as built (book 108)

- **The cast is paid by hand so the counter stays castable.** With a counterspell held, the pilot taps the lands the counter does not need before it casts (`agents/reserve.ts`; the `tapForMana` actions the engine already offers a person). The engine's automatic payment is unchanged.
- **A play that leaves the counter payable keeps the hold.** Before, only passing kept the hold bonus, so a land drop or a cheap spell beside a held counter read as giving the counter up.
- **A control deck weighs the hold three times as much on its own turn.** Other archetypes keep the S9 weight (a midrange list with four counters gained nothing at any multiple).
- **Measured** (1,500 games a list, each game paired with its old-pilot twin by seed):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| the Locks | 33.0% | 36.8% | **+3.8 ± 2.0** | 3.35 → 3.63 |
| the Undertow | 32.8% | 34.9% | **+2.1 ± 1.4** | 0.92 → 0.97 |

## Broader sideboarding as built (Part 2)

- **Rules 5–8** in `world/sideboard-ai.ts`, after the first four, each keyed on the opponent's registered list:
  5. **Sweepers** against fifteen or more creatures of power two or less, and at least twice our own count of small creatures (a weenie deck does not sweep its own board).
  6. **Creature counters** against twenty or more creatures: Essence Scatter in for a general counter, never the reverse; rule 3 leaves them in.
  7. **A steal** against three or more copies of creatures rated 2.5 or better: Control Magic in.
  8. **Blockers:** against eight or more fliers, reach or flying blockers of four mana or less; otherwise, against twelve or more creatures of mana value two or less, defenders.
- **Implementer's thresholds** (the brief gave none): eight fliers; twelve cheap creatures for "an aggressive list"; four mana for a blocker; two cards a shape.
- **The field's fifteen** reserves slots for them (`SIDEBOARD_SHAPE_S56`: graveyards 4, relics 3, creature removal 3, sweepers 2, creature counters 2, a steal 2, blockers 2, then the best of the rest).
- **Fixed on the way:** rule 3 was bringing graveyard exile in as "the best playable left" (the Kiln swapped a Tormod's Crypt for a Counterspell against the Coin); a shape never takes out a card that only makes mana (the Locks were cutting the Lotus for Control Magic).
- **Measured** (each list's sideboarded sixty against the others' registered sixties, 450 games a list, paired with the plain run):

| | mean gain from sideboarding |
|---|---|
| the S55 rules | +3.0 points |
| with rules 5–8 | **+3.4 points** |
| each shape alone, over the S55 rules | sweepers +0.2 · creature counters +0.3 · a steal +0.7 · blockers 0.0 |

- The gain from the four shapes is small and inside the noise. None costs anything. The steal is the one with a visible effect (the Undertow +4.7 ± 3.6, the Tally +2.7 ± 2.3).
- **The brief's check — the Kiln against the Pall** (300 games, the Pall sideboarded): 35% as registered → 42% under the S55 rule (four hate cards for its four lowest-rated cards, the Pyromancers) → **59%** with the walls leaving first. A person's plan measured 60%.
- **Rule 3 was tested off** for Constructed (counterspells never leave). It was worse: the Locks' gain from sideboarding fell from +7.6 to +1.6 and the Kiln's from +4.2 to +1.3. It stays on.

## Protocol (Chris, 2026-10-06, after the session proper)

Chris delivered a new custom card with its art and printed face:

> **Protocol** — {U}{U} Enchantment — Aura. Enchant creature. Enchanted creature gets −2/−0. You control enchanted creature as long as its power would be 0 or less if you didn't control it.

- **In the pool** (246 → 247), **tier 2**. Chris allowed tier 2 or 3; it measures below the tier-3 Control Magic in every slot tried.
- **One new word (R-105):** a control static conditional on the enchanted creature's power (`{ref: "attachedPower"}`, and `atMost` on a static condition).
- **Fuzz first:** 768 full-tier games beside anthems, pump Auras, Control Magic, Lumen, bounce and Aura removal. No exception; replays byte-exact. Then 8 fixtures.
- **The AI** prices it as a steal on a creature the shrink brings to 0 and as a partial shrink otherwise (book 110). It casts it 0.4–1.1 times a game.
- **`open:rr --swap` now takes a variant** (`protocol~cost={1}{U}~shrink=3`), so a card's parameters can be tested in a real list.

**The sensitivity grid.** Four costs × three shrink sizes, in five slots of four lists, each cell 1,500 games paired by seed with the round-robin of record (93,000 games; the full tables are in `results/s56/protocol-grid.md`, untracked). The change in the list's win rate, averaged over the five slots:

| cost | −1/−0 | −2/−0 | −3/−0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| {U} | -1.7 | +2.5 | +5.0 |
| {U}{U} | -3.5 | +0.2 | +2.3 |
| {1}{U} | -2.4 | +1.9 | +4.2 |
| {1}{U}{U} | -3.8 | -1.0 | +1.1 |

The card as printed ({U}{U}, −2/−0), slot by slot:

| slot | Protocol as printed | Control Magic in the same slot |
|---|---|---|
| the Kiln: in place of its 4 Control Magic | −6.2 ± 2.0 | (the baseline) |
| the Depths: in place of its 2 Control Magic | −3.4 ± 1.6 | (the baseline) |
| the Kiln: in place of 4 Tidewall, beside Control Magic | +4.5 ± 1.9 | not run |
| the Locks: in place of 2 Tidewall + 2 Ponder | +2.2 ± 2.1 | +11.0 ± 2.2 |
| the Undertow: in place of 3 Boomerang | +3.9 ± 1.7 | +8.9 ± 1.8 |

What the grid says:
- **As printed it is a fair card: an addition, not a replacement.** It improves a list when it takes a weak slot and costs the list when it replaces Control Magic.
- **The shrink is the strong dial.** Each point of it is worth about 2 to 4 points of win rate. At −1/−0 the card is a liability; at −3/−0 it approaches Control Magic at half the cost.
- **The second blue pip matters more than the mana.** {1}{U} measures about 1.5 points better than {U}{U} at the same mana value, and nearly as well as {U}. This is Concern 1 again (these lists are short of blue).
- **Nothing in the grid is broken.** The strongest cell tried ({U}, −3/−0) is +5.0 on average and still below Control Magic where both were run.

**The wording was settled the same day (Chris).** The first face read "As long as enchanted creature's power is 0 or less, you control enchanted creature." A creature's power can depend on who controls it (an anthem, Clio), and that text has no stable answer then: taken, its power rises; released, it falls. The engine had been built to read the power the creature would have if we did not control it, and Chris reworded the card to say so. Nothing in the engine or the grid changed.
- An opponent's Glorious Anthem keeps their 2/2 safe (3 − 2 = 1); our own Anthem does not hand a taken 2/2 back.
- With our Clio, a 3/3 under Protocol is taken at her first depth counter and kept (a fixture pins it). This is the synergy the card was written for.
- Chris considered a stronger version (the creature comes over at full power) and kept the shrink, so the card is not a two-mana Control Magic beside Clio.
- The corrected face (no power/toughness box, the new text) is wired.
