## Chris's rulings at the kickoff (2026-10-04)

1. **Two difficulties: Normal and Hard.** "Easy" goes (it was identical to standard once the entrance went flat). A saved event marked easy or standard loads as Normal.
2. **The day scale.** The four-day Convocation: Day 1 ×0, Day 2 ×0.5, Days 3–4 and the Umbel ×1. A single event (one Draft, Sealed or Open, five rounds and the Umbel) is one full day, ×1. The short Convocation (a draft, then a Constructed day): Day 1 ×0, its last day and the Umbel ×1 — the climb always ends at full strength.
3. **ADR-158 amended — every pilot is master, on Normal too.** Chris went 19–0 twice against an all-master field; the AI is not turned down. The seat's pilot is recorded (master) for when that changes. The field's spread comes from its builders (Constructed: stock / light / heavy tinkerers; Limited: rating noise σ 0 / 0.2 / 0.4 by thirds). **To test at the next large simulation:** journeyman pilots against master on a few decks — whether journeyman has any play against master. Unless it does, master all the way.
4. **ADR-158's measure:** deck quality against finish, as briefed; if journeyman pilots ever join the field, pilot level and a combined seat strength are reported beside it.
