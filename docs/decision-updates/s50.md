## Rules against the brief

- **"The Kavu is cast regardless" does not hold on an empty table.** Its trigger is mandatory and targets "creature": with no creature across the table it must target one of ours, and with none of ours, itself (CR 603.3d → 601.2c: a target is chosen if one can be) — a four-mana 4/2 that shoots itself. The AI holds it then, unless one of ours would survive the four. With any creature across the table it is cast whatever it can kill, as the brief says.
