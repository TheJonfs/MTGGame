## Part 1 — The vintage audit

### How the vintage is counted
- **The pool's size is the loader's count** (320 today: tokens and laws included), so `poolAt` and the pool count in the handoff are the same number.
- **A card's place in the pool** is the commit that first held its def. `data/convocation/pool-history.json` records, for each card, that commit's date and the pool's size once it landed. The vintage test fails when a new card has no row.
- **A list's `poolAt`** is the pool's size at the commit that first held the list in its format's file. The Open's contributed lists older than 2026-10-12 (the day the file moved) take the date in their source line. The Open's seeds are at 260, the pool after S50.
- **The brief's "244" for the Open's seeds is 260 here.** 244 is the registry's count after S50, which leaves out the sixteen token defs. The session is the same one; only the convention differs. Nothing Dear's 315 agrees.

### Ported lists (Chris's ruling, 2026-11-18)
A port is a mechanical swap, not a fresh read of the pool. So a ported list carries two things:
- `poolAt`: the pool when it was registered in this format, as the brief ruled;
- `portOf`: the list it came from (`"open:levy"`).

The audit reads a list at **the vintage of the root of its `portOf` chain**. Nothing Dear's Levy is `poolAt` 315 and is read at 260 (the Open's seed). Without this, the four Nothing Dear cliff lists would each have had a delta of five cards.
- **A port is "whole", or whole but for its mana, its Power or the format's rule.** A list the source line calls "built by the implementer", or a revival that added cards (Esper's Locks), is its own root.
- 102 of the 187 lists are ports.

### The mechanical first cut (`pnpm vintage-audit`)
Per list: `poolAt`, the vintage it is read at, its match rate of record, the list by mana value, and the **delta**. A delta card is one added after the vintage that:
- is legal in the format beside the list (one copy added to the sixty passes the format's rule);
- is castable in the list's colours (a hybrid card for either of its colours, ADR-197);
- costs no more than the top of the list's curve.

A land is kept whatever it makes and is read by hand. A colour the list holds fewer than three cards of is a splash (Emerald Snakes' Time Walk) and brings no candidates.

### The trial row
`zsh analysis/s73/trial.sh <format> <list> <tag> '<from:n:to>'`: the list as registered and the list with the swap, each against its format's whole field, 160 matches a pairing, seed 91, the same seed in both arms. `analysis/s73/swap-read.py` pairs the two arms match by match and prints the three columns.
- **The gain** is the mean of the per-match differences, ± 1.96 standard errors of those differences.
- **The bar** is ADR-168: +2 or more with a positive lower bound, and no pairing lost clear of its error.
- **The second seed** (Chris, 2026-11-18): a row that clears the bar under +5 is re-run on seed 173 before it is called a clear. A row at +5 or more stands on one seed.
- Trials ran under `card-rating.json` v1.5 and pilot 176. The tables of record are v1.4, so a trial's base arm does not equal the recorded table. Both arms of a trial share the ratings, so the gain is unaffected.
