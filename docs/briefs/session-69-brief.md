## Part 2 — The defence (three rules; probe → rule → ladder)
- **The block that loses** (`blockloss`, book 146): from the racing probe, the pilot's extra blocks (its choice 3.6% to Chris's 32.1% in the seven positions). A block is refused when it spends a creature to save life we can afford to lose — the life saved is less than the blocker's worth to the race, read by the crack-back and the clock (our turns to kill against theirs); chump blocks stay when the clock says we are the one dying. Measure: the defending lists against the Rabble in Grixis and Jeskai (the Writ, Clio, the Locks, the Aerie, the Watch), paired; the Rabble's numbers in both tables after.
- **The sweeper that waits** (`sweepwait`, book 147): book 117's shape for a sweeper — Wrath, Pyroclasm, the Wave, Retribution, Savage Twister wait for a board worth the card (the sum of the creatures it kills against ours, at or above the card's worth) unless the race forces it (the clock says we lose before a better board comes). Measure: the Jeskai Sweepers (30.8), the Locks, the Chancel, Mardu Control; sweeps per game and the board each met (the probe's reading: 64% into one or none → ?); the ladder.
- **Removal under our own payoff** (`payoffcost`, book 148): a removal spell or ability aimed at a creature of *ours* (a Kavu's trigger, a Grenade's sacrifice, a Cairnbrand's cost) — and a kill of theirs while *their* death payoff is out (Blood Artist, the Massacre, the Usher) — is charged the life the payoff takes from us; refused when that life is the game. The `deathCharge` read applied to the controller of a removal's target. Measure on the Massacre and the Usher pairings (Chris's game 17 as the fixture: at 1 life the Grenade at Meliyan is refused); the ladder.
