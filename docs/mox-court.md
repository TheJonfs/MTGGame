# The Mox Court — five custom guardians for the five Moxen (Chris-designed; planner-audited)

*Born of the great swap (five-powers-design §6): the real legends moved to the power-dungeons; the Moxen gain a matched gem-titled custom court. Cycle constraints: mono-colour, guardian-class (the Rorix-to-Titania complexity band; zero-to-two-rider bills), one or two lines each, ADR-052/053 ceremony + printed JPGs per the pipeline. Decks planner-drafted at Moxen-class strength once the court stands; guardian-sim tunes them. Sole-mechanism drop law applies (each card drops only from its bearer's defeat).*

## Entry 1 — The Emerald Keeper (Mox Emerald, the Deepwood)

**The Emerald Keeper** — {X}{X}{G}{G} Legendary Creature — Hydra, 2/2
- Trample
- When The Emerald Keeper enters, put X +1/+1 counters on each creature you control.

*Chris-drafted; planner-endorsed as written.*

**Design notes (recorded):**
- **Double-X is load-bearing**: every team-wide point costs two mana (single-X at six mana would be Overrun-and-a-body). Rate: X=1 → 4 mana, 3/3 trampler +1/+1 around; X=2 → 6 mana, 4/4 + team +2/+2; X=3 → 8 mana, 5/5 + team +3/+3.
- **Self-inclusion**: "each creature you control" catches the Keeper (on the battlefield as his own trigger resolves) — the printed 2/2 is really (2+X)/(2+X) trample.
- X=0 is legal and harmless ({G}{G} 2/2 legend).
- **The naming brush, chosen knowingly**: Keeper ≈ Warden; the gem title disambiguates, and garden-keeper vs prison-warden are opposite jobs — the echo accepted.
- **AI**: X-choice rides Blaze's enumeration precedent; the existing counter valuation scores board-count × X. No pins expected.

**Bill (two small riders — the cycle's opening cost, flagged honestly):**
1. **Double-X cost support** — the cost model gains an X-count (announce charges 2X); representation extension on the Blaze/Drana machinery.
2. **`{ref: xPaid}`** — the announced X persists on the permanent for its ETB trigger to read (Blaze's X is a spell amount and vanishes; a permanent's must ride the object). Ref-family member seven.
Everything else is paid for: scoped team-counters are the Season's shipped machinery (S22) plus the ref amount; trample ancient.

## Entry 2 — The Sapphire Sage (Mox Sapphire, the Drowned Reach)

**The Sapphire Sage** — {1}{U}{U}{U}{U} Legendary Creature — Faerie Wizard, 2/2
- Flash, flying
- When The Sapphire Sage enters, for each player, choose target permanent that player controls. Return each of those permanents to their owner's hands.

*Chris-drafted; planner-endorsed as written. **Bill: zero** — compiles in a duel to two mandatory targets with controller predicates (Aetherbolt's machinery). Notes: the Sage is a legal target for his own trigger — **Sage-bounces-Sage is a repeatable 5-mana flash tempo engine**, discovered not printed; the quadruple-U is the commitment tax. AI: own-side target choice rides the blink classification (ETB value or the Sage himself); watch-level. Joins the faerie thread.*

## Entry 3 — The Jet Witch (Mox Jet, the Barrowlands)

**The Jet Witch** — {B}{B}{B} Legendary Creature — Human Shaman, 2/2
- Pay 2 life: Draw a card.

*Chris-drafted; planner-endorsed as written. Greed without the mana — degenerate at constructed's 20 life, **self-balancing at Cinquefoil's scale in both directions**: a genuine engine atop a guardian's boss-class pool (she reads fortunes in her own blood); a knife that cuts the wielder at player world-life ~10 (20% per card). **Bill: one rider** — life as an activation cost (the A9/Purge pay-life primitive priced into tap-ability costs). Pins: pin-17 family — never at life ≤ 2; a draw-discipline heuristic (hand size × life floor) tuned by guardian-sim.*

## Entry 4 — The Ruby Tyrant (Mox Ruby, the Shattered Caldera)

**The Ruby Tyrant** — {2}{R}{R}{R} Legendary Creature — Dragon, 4/4
- Flying, haste
- {2}{R}: The Ruby Tyrant deals 2 damage to any target and 1 damage to you.

*Chris-drafted; planner-endorsed as written. **Bill: zero** — Aetherbolt's two-effect shape + the Gallows Djinn's self-damage. Shivan's grandchild: a notch above Serra/the Gale, paid for by the recoil (five pulls = half a world-life). Pin: the Djinn's sibling — never at life ≤ 1. Two dragons in the world is correct cosmology post-swap; rubies breed dragons.*

## Entry 5 — The Pearl Cleric (Mox Pearl, the Reliquary Wastes)

**The Pearl Cleric** — {1}{W}{W} Legendary Creature — Human Cleric, 3/3
- {W}, Exile the top card of your library: You gain 1 life.
- {W}{W}, Exile the top two cards of your library: Target creature gains indestructible until end of turn.

*Chris-drafted; planner-endorsed as written. She pays in your own future — exiled tops are cards never seen, and overuse walks toward DECKED. **The Witch's mirror twin**: black spends life for cards; white spends cards for life. The indestructible mode is live counterplay against the Wrath decks (Reya's, the Warden's). **Bill: one rider + a possible half** — exile-top-as-activation-cost (parameterized count); keyword-grant-until-EOT flagged in case word 3's haste grant and the pump-duration machinery never met. Pins: library floor (never exile below N); watch — responding with indestructible to a stack-pending destroy (flash-response machinery exists; valuation is the question).*

**The court stands complete.** Register confirmed across five: every card charges its wielder — double-X, own-side bounce, blood, recoil, the future. The lords tax the opponent's verbs; the court taxes its own bearer. Final bill: ~4.5 riders. Vocations final: Cleric, Sage, Witch, Tyrant, Keeper.
