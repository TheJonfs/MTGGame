## Part 2 — Two books from saved games (ADR-167: the probe is the first deliverable of each)
- **Mystic Snake never counters** (concern 30; 3 counters in ~120 casts). *Probe*: from `--save-games` (Countersnake and Bant Countersnake against Mardu Control, Mono-Black Clio, Reaper Clio), every Snake cast — the phase, whose turn, what was on the stack, what the opponent cast in the next two turns that it could have countered, and the mana the list had open at the opponent's end step. *The rule the planner expects* (`snakehold`): a flash creature with a counter rider is held by the counter rule's logic (book 108's hold against the list's plan read), and is cast as a body only when the board needs it now, when the opponent's list holds nothing worth the counter, or **at the opponent's end step when no target came** (the mana otherwise unspent). *Measure*: every registered list holding the Snake, paired, 160 matches a pairing against the three above and the field; Bodies' and the Open's tables re-run if a row moves; the ladder.
- **Vindicate at lands** (concern 31; ~70 of 161 against Countersnake hit the grounds and duals). *Probe*: each Vindicate's target by kind, the best creature target standing at the time and its price, and the game's result; grouped by the opponent's list shape (a creature deck; a Valakut list, where the grounds *are* the target). *The rule, if the probe says the land hit comes while a creature worth more stands* (`landhit` gated): book 162's bonus applies only when no creature target exceeds the land's worth by a margin (the probe names it). *Measure*: Mardu Control against Countersnake, Bant Countersnake, Emerald Snakes, Reaper Clio (the creature decks) *and* against Earthrise, the Minefield and the Pinnacle (where it must not lose the grounds); paired, 160; the ladder.
- **The baseline leak** (concern 32): a census, not a rule — every effect that can resolve to nothing (a counter with no spell, an exile with no card, a destroy whose filter empties, an exchange with no cross-side pair) and whether the scorer's positive baseline carries it to a cast. The list goes in the handoff; the planner rules which become books.
