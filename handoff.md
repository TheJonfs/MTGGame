# Handoff — after Session 16 (2026-08-23; radial world landed the same day)

## State of the world

**Roaming visibility is real.** Encounter rolls are gone: every opponent instance is a **roamer** with a map position that moves each player step — toward you within sight, away when your renown says flee, random drift otherwise; contact (same cell, whoever stepped) = parley; **any parley outcome removes the roamer** (Chris's ruling; lair residents leave only when defeated); regions below their density **respawn** one roamer per clock tick, out of sight. The map is 2× linear (80×56 from a `mapScale` knob), towns are per-region by density (every non-wild region ≥1), the player **starts in their colour's town**, and the world UI is a **29×19 viewport** centred on the player with a **minimap**, roamer chips (fleeing marked), and a sight diamond. **`world-save-v3`** carries roamer positions, **multiple decks** (`decks` + `activeDeckName`, picker in the editor: new/duplicate/switch/delete; spares subtract the active deck only), an append-only **provenance** log, `renown`, `starterId`; v1/v2 saves migrate through the chain. **Five authored 30-card mono starters** (`data/world/starters.json`, one-drops in, easy/hard variants) are the new-game choice; slice decks A–E are enemy/ladder infrastructure. Engine: **`mill`** (R-046) and the **creature mana-producer summoning-sickness gate** (R-047); **Llanowar Elves** and **Cathartic Adept** encoded (Scryfall re-verified, art fetched), pool 70→72. **The radial world (ADR-072) landed in the same session**: the planner's ADR + content drop arrived mid-session; catalog **v1** carries the 15 named regions (colour × tier), their towns, and the five strongholds; the generator places five seed-jittered colour spokes × three rings from an elliptically-normalised centre, strongholds at r≈0.92 (fixed points, sealed until S19+), towns per region by ring-tiered density named hub-outward (the first civilized name is the home-start town — Whitewell, Greyharbor, Lowmarket, Forgeton, Elderbriar), **roads** (shortest paths over a town MST + the hub cycle) with **terrain-keyed roamer speed** (`roamerStepsPerPlayerStep`, road 0.5), lairs per wild region, and the reserved **`explored`** bitfield (home region + everything ever in sight; fog rendering deferred per the ADR). Fuzz clean (2,000 starter games + the standard tiers), **226/228 tests, FUZZ_FULL exit 0**; the generator fuzz (200 seeds) asserts the ADR-072 invariants.
