# Token art: wurm_6_6_b_trample (Wurm Token, black)

Card-art class (S7 feedback round / S17 Expansion 1: tokens get our own art
rather than Scryfall pulls — they are custom defs, so oracle grounding
doesn't apply). House style like the goblin/soldier/bear/wurm/beast tokens.
Rendered 1:1, center-cropped to the frame's 5:4 art window (35% upward bias).

S64 (Chris): the 6/6 black Wurm with trample that Penumbra Wurm leaves when it
dies (S62, ADR-177) — the one creature token without art. Kept distinct from
the green 4/4 Wurm token (a mossy wurm rearing from the forest floor): this
one is the dead wurm's shadow given a body — dusk-dark, smoke at its edges,
the ground scorched grey where it moves.

## Visual descriptor (locked)

```text
A single enormous black wurm surging forward out of a wall of dusk shadow,
seen from slightly below and to one side: a long armoured serpentine body of
dark slate plates, its outline fraying into drifting black smoke where it
leaves the shadow, a broad eyeless head with a gaping jaw ringed in pale
teeth, the earth cracked and ash-grey beneath it. Twilight sky low and violet
behind, a few bare dead trees, no rider, no people. The head and front coils
fill the upper center, the body trailing back into darkness.
```
