**ADR-085 — S23 ratifications.**

*Ratified as shipped:* the kickoff corrections (Thundersnake at the formula's 18g — the spec's 12g was a planner error, Bolt's price riding along; first-collector wirings counted as skeleton per the R-061 precedent, not vocabulary bill); the Thundersnake's big-blocker gate as shipped (deliberately blind to deathtouch-under-toughness and flying-only boards — the cheap check now, refinement on evidence); the Djinn's flat tax pricing (hard never-lethal pins hold the felt-wrong line; life-scaled pricing is tuning territory if tables ever ask); the module-level audio singleton with its HMR double-evaluation quirk (harmless, flagged); the start-outside-the-gate offset with scan-order determinism (a seeded pick is one line if cross-seed sameness ever reads wrong); ford marks kept as pure flavor; the terrain-variety sprite expansion; END_STEP and BLOCKS collectors with eventContext.

*The rivers reversal (recorded as doctrine):* the planner's impassable-with-bridges design is **superseded by Chris's flavor ruling** — play evidence (a map-length hunt for one crossing) beat the routing-texture argument, and the reachability-repair machinery went with the barrier. The generalized lesson enters the record: **on a step-economy map, geography suggests and never taxes** — the world's friction budget belongs to its systems (clocks, sieges, lords), not its scenery. River-source clustering (concern 1) is accepted as-is; stratified sources (one per map third, or spoke-anchored) is the shelved answer if an "every world feels riverine" want emerges.

*Riding the audio mapping round:* the stronghold-vs-dungeon duel-music distinction (concern 3) and the unlock-race nit (concern 5); `docs/audio-cues-s23.md` is the round's worksheet.
