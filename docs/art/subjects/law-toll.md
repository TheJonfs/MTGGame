# Law art: law_toll (The Toll — the Furnace Gate's law)

S22 playtest r1: law piece for the battlefield token frame. Every spell the
intruder casts, the Gate taxes in fire. House style. Rendered 1:1, 5:4 crop.

## Visual descriptor (locked)

```text
A massive brass toll-brazier built into a black basalt gate, its bowl
brimming with white-hot coals, a channel of molten gold running from a
coin-slot in the gate's arch down into the fire, sparks leaping with a
snap toward the viewer, heat-shimmer warping the air, embers drifting
```
