## Concerns

1. **The lords' ante exposure (pre-existing, found by construction).** `game.setAside` takes the top ante cards with no prizeOnly filter, and `applyInteriorDuel` escrows `facts.ante[1]` verbatim — a lord's deck stakes his own card ×3 as readily as anything else, so a stronghold win can pay a SECOND lord card through escrow, against ADR-081's sole channel. The Corolla filters it (`anteWithheld`); the strongholds and the court (their cards ×3) don't. One filter in `applyInteriorDuel` (needs the pool) closes it; ruling wanted on whether the engine's `setAside` should skip prizeOnly instead (cleaner: a sole-mechanism card is never a stake for either side — but that also means the PLAYER's Moxen/lord cards can't be ante'd away, which may be the intent anyway).
2. **The petal-world's scale and pace.** 41×41 with the viewport at 29×19: the flower does not fit on one screen (the minimap shows it whole). Tip-to-tip walks are ~35 cells; with no clock the walk is pure travel. Chris's verdict wanted: shrink (`corollaGridSize` 33?) or keep the scale and add a "walk to the tip" affordance (the rail already has one per petal).
3. **The petals meet at the base.** The lobes touch each other near the heart (no paper gap) so the inner ring reads as five quadrants meeting at a disc; the outer ends read as petals. A narrower lobe (b 0.21 → 0.17 of R) or a longer stem would open gaps — register verdict first.
