# Guardian portrait: Drana, Kalastria Bloodchief (Jet Barrow, B)

S20 playtest r2 (Chris): in-battle portraits for the five Mox guardians —
mage-portrait law (`docs/prompts/portraits.md`). Signature-card rule:
recognisably Drana (the pooled card's lineage — the pale noblewoman in
bladed dark court dress with a bead-hung crown), consistent with, not
copied from, the printed art.

*Refusal history (r2–r3): the first two descriptors were refused
(PROHIBITED_CONTENT). Chris isolated the trigger by hand via the chat
interface: the phrase "two great curved black horns" — striking it
rendered cleanly. The locked descriptor below is that corrected wording
(the horn crown became a dark filigree crown). Chris's own render of it
(16:9, via the chat interface) was picked over the in-pipeline render
and is the canonical — `source-chris.jpeg` in the image folder is the
original file; the UI portrait is a square crop.*

## Visual descriptor (locked)

```text
Head-and-shoulders portrait, three-quarter view: a pale, ageless
noblewoman of dark court elegance, chin high, a faint knowing smile;
sleek black hair beneath an ornate dark filigree crown hung with fine
chains of beads; a high collar of layered petal-like black plates over
a formal gown; one gloved hand raised with a wisp of dark smoke curling
above the fingers. Expression imperious and readable at 96 pixels.
Exactly two watercolor washes: deep violet-black and parchment cream;
all else ink line and unpainted paper. No background scenery.
```
