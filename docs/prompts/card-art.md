# Card art prompts (ADR-052)

Card illustrations are **exempt from style.md** — do not prepend its preamble. Each custom card gets four candidates in distinct styles/compositions, specified here by the planner; Chris picks. Common constraints only: no text, no borders/frame, painterly-print-plausible (nothing photoreal or 3D-render), composition readable in a 5:4 crop, single focal figure/action unless stated.

## cunning_tactician — "Cunning Tactician" (W creature: commander who taps threats down)

1. **Classical oil** — "Oil painting in the warm classical fantasy style of 1990s trading-card art: a human soldier-commander on a battlefield ridge at dawn, gold-and-white tabard, one arm extended in a decisive command, banners behind, warm rimlight."
2. **Ink-and-gouache action** — "High-contrast ink drawing with gouache highlights: a soldier mid-stride raising an open hand in a halt order, strong diagonal composition, motion in the cloak, limited palette of white, steel and a red accent."
3. **Watercolor storybook** — "Muted watercolor: a tactician leaning over a candlelit map table in a command tent, markers and counters on the map, thoughtful expression, soft edges, quiet cerebral mood."
4. **Chiaroscuro portrait** — "Dramatic chiaroscuro three-quarter portrait: an armored tactician against a dark ground, single hard light source, calculating gaze, fine armor detail, near-monochrome with warm skin tones."

## gaean_wurm — "Gaean Wurm" (G creature, custom #2: a wurm that grows with the forests you control — trample, +1/+1 per Forest)

Written by the implementer at Chris's request (S17, after the card landed; the S18 brief assigned the entry to the planner — adjust freely). The idea to carry in every direction: the wurm *is* the forest — its size comes from the land, moss and roots on its hide, trees as the scale reference.

1. **Classical oil** — "Oil painting in the warm classical fantasy style of 1990s trading-card art: a colossal green wurm rearing from an old-growth forest floor, moss and ferns growing on its ridged hide, roots trailing from its coils, ancient trees dwarfed beside it, golden late-afternoon light through the canopy."
2. **Ink-and-gouache action** — "High-contrast ink drawing with gouache highlights: a huge wurm bursting through a treeline mid-charge, trunks splintering, earth thrown up, strong diagonal composition, limited palette of forest greens, umber and bone-white."
3. **Watercolor storybook** — "Muted watercolor: a vast wurm asleep and coiled among giant forest roots, its mossy back nearly indistinguishable from a green ridge, small deer grazing on it unaware, soft dappled light, quiet enormous mood."
4. **Woodcut print** — "Bold two-colour woodcut / linocut print: the sinuous coils of a great wurm winding between stylised tree trunks, heavy black carved line, flat forest green and cream paper, strong rhythmic pattern of coils and trunks."
