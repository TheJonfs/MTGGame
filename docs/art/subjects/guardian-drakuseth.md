# Guardian portrait: Drakuseth, Maw of Flames (Ruby Caldera, R)

S20 playtest r2 (Chris): in-battle portraits for the five Mox guardians.
Drakuseth is a dragon, so the bestiary variant of the portrait law
applies: a distinctive three-quarter bust, silhouette-strong, two washes.
Signature-card rule: recognisably Drakuseth (CMM #535 lineage — the
storm-dark dragon with a jagged spined crest, fire from more than one
quarter), consistent with, not copied from, the printed art.

## Visual descriptor (locked)

```text
Three-quarter bust of a colossal dragon, head turned toward the viewer:
dark ridged scales, a jagged crest of swept-back horns and blade spines,
burning eyes; the maw open mid-roar with flame beginning to spill
between the teeth, and two smaller gouts of fire flaring from the wing
joints behind its shoulders; smoke rising in thin ink whorls. Menace
readable at 96 pixels. Exactly two watercolor washes: ember orange-red
and parchment cream; all else ink line and unpainted paper. No
background scenery.
```
