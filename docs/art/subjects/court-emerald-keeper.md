# Court portrait: The Emerald Keeper (Mox Emerald, Deepwood, G)

S25 (ADR-088): bestiary three-quarter bust (the Drakuseth precedent).
Custom legend. Register: the hydra whose growth is shared — a
garden-keeper, not a warden; every head another season.

## Visual descriptor (locked)

```text
Three-quarter bust of a young hydra: five serpentine heads rising from
one broad trunk like stalks in a bed, the central head inclined toward
the viewer with a calm gardener's patience, the others turned outward at
watchful angles; scales of mossy green flecked with small emerald
glints; flowering vines twined loosely around the necks with a few small
white blossoms. Bearing tended and tending, silhouette readable at 96
pixels. Exactly two watercolor washes: emerald moss green and parchment
cream; all else ink line and unpainted paper. No background scenery.
```
