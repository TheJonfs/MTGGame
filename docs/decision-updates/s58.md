## Part 2 — the Cinder diff: the tool is built; the report waits for recorded games

- **The 8–0 export cannot be diffed.** It records each game's seed and result, not its moves.
- **`pnpm play-diff <file…>`** is built. For each recorded game it replays to every decision the human made, asks the pilot (the master AI on the same deck, seeing the same view), names each difference by kind, and plays both lines out from there with the pilot at the helm (`--playouts`, the same seeds): the human's choice against the pilot's own. A decision is a unit: a hand-tapped payment with its cast; an attack as its whole set; a block as its whole set.
- **The engine piece is `replayThenPlay`** (a logged game replayed to a decision and played on by agents, with fresh randomness from there), with a test.
- **The player's games are now recorded.** Each finished game of a Convocation is kept in the browser under its own key (its spec, its actions and its randomness; the newest thirty; never in the event's save). The door shows **"download my recorded games (n)"**, and that file is what `play-diff` reads.
- **Checked on a stand-in** (the journeyman piloting the Cinder against the Levy): 24 decisions with a choice, 3 differences, named and played out. It says nothing about Chris's play.
- **What is needed:** Chris plays the Cinder again (any event) and downloads the file.
