## Chris's rulings at the kickoff (2026-10-06)

- **The full Convocation seats 128** (sixteen pods of eight), not the brief's thirty-two. Chris: a sixteen-round event wants a field of 100–200 so the Swiss does not rematch. Measured on the event's own pairer, 100 random sixteen-round events per row:

| seats | draft rounds paired room-wide | in-pod, second pods random | in-pod, second pods by standings |
|---|---|---|---|
| 32 | no rematch, but pairings 0.49 wins apart on average; the search up to 141,000 steps | 17% of events rematch | 91% (5 a event) |
| 64 | 0 · 0.17 wins apart | 1% | 29% |
| **128** | 0 · 0.07 | 0% | **1%** |
| 160 | 0 · 0.05 | 0% | 0% |

- **Draft rounds are paired inside the pod** (MTR 7.6: "Players within a pod may play only against other players within that pod"); the Constructed rounds pair across the room.
- **The second draft's pods are formed by standings** (the top eight records draft together, and down). MTR 7.6's text says pods are "random drafting circles"; by standings is the Pro Tour's Day-2 practice as I understand it — **not verified against a published Pro Tour document**.
- Recorded, not adopted: MTR 10.4 has Pro Tour competitors who reach the Top 8's match-point mark early locked in with byes for the remaining Swiss rounds. Not built; the Umbel takes the eight by the final standings.
- The MTR names "the Swiss pairing algorithm" without detail (10.4); the no-rematch rule is the algorithm's and the pairing software's practice, not a written MTR line.
